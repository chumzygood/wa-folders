Where Weka does more

Weka's listing and site cover a sales pipeline: you can split a tab into stages such as Leads, Quoted and Won, and open a board to drag chats between stages. It can schedule a message to send later, including with attachments, and wekacrm.app also lists broadcasts to a whole tab. AI features can summarise a chat, suggest replies, or translate a message or draft, using Chrome's built-in AI on your computer by default, or your own OpenAI or Anthropic key. If a reminder is due while your computer is off, Weka says it emails you. Signing in syncs your tabs, notes and quick replies to another computer. The site lists six languages, including right-to-left, and a larger view at teams.wekacrm.app.

Where WA Folders does more

WA Folders has no account and no WA Folders server. Folders, notes and the search index stay in Chrome on your computer. The only network uses are ones you turn on: optional Chrome sync on Pro (off by default), your own AI key, the Pro license check, and a one-time speech model download from Hugging Face the first time you turn a voice note into text (you are asked first). It can search chat names, your notes and messages it has already seen, and export the open chat to HTML, CSV, Markdown or JSON. It can also turn voice notes into text on your computer. It never sends a message for you, and Pro is $4.99 once.

FAQ

Is WA Folders a Weka alternative?

Yes, for tabs, notes and reminders. Weka also schedules messages and, on its site, lists broadcasts. WA Folders also searches and exports chats, with no account.

Is Weka really free?

Its listing and site say every feature is free. The Chrome Web Store Details section also flags "Offers in-app purchases". Weka's site says they tried paid plans, turned them off, and will give notice if that changes.

Does Weka store my WhatsApp messages?

Its listing and privacy policy say message content is never uploaded. They do store what you create in Weka, including tab names, notes, reminders and scheduled messages, and identifiers for chats you put in tabs.

Do I need an account for WA Folders?

No. Folders and notes stay in Chrome on your computer. Pro can optionally sync between your Chrome browsers, off by default.

Can I use both?

Both add a tab strip above the WhatsApp Web chat list, so they may clash. We have not tested them together.
