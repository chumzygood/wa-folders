Meta AI chats showing up in your WhatsApp chat list? How to find, hide or delete them

Published November 4, 2026

Until now, everything you asked Meta AI on WhatsApp lived inside one conversation. WhatsApp is changing that. On November 4, 2026, WABetaInfo reported that WhatsApp beta for Android 2.26.44.6 lets some testers create separate Meta AI chats, give each one a name and a profile picture, and see them in the main chat list next to their normal conversations.

If you've spotted an AI chat you don't remember making, or you want to know how to get rid of these, here is what's going on and what you can do.

What's changing

In an earlier beta (2.26.9.4), WhatsApp added threads inside the Meta AI conversation. Each thread kept its own context, and you reached them from a panel inside the Meta AI chat. WABetaInfo says the new version turns those threads into full chats:

  1. Inside the chat with Meta AI, a new option in the three-dot menu creates a new chat.
  2. You type a name for it, for example "Trip to Lisbon" or "Recipes", and can pick a profile picture.
  3. The new chat appears in your chat list. It has the Meta AI logo over its profile icon, so you can tell it apart from a person.
  4. A new AI filter at the top of the Chats tab shows only these AI chats.

A Meta AI chat only gets created when you start one. WhatsApp describes Meta AI as an optional service, and Meta only receives the messages you send to it or mention @Meta AI in. This is also still a beta test on Android. WABetaInfo says it's reaching more testers over the coming weeks, and WhatsApp hasn't said when everyone gets it. There's no word yet on iPhone, WhatsApp Web or the desktop apps.

How to hide the AI filter

According to WABetaInfo, the AI filter is optional. You can move it out of the main chat list into a separate section if you'd rather keep the top of your chats clean. The report doesn't give the exact menu path, and the option only exists for testers who have the feature, so if you don't see an AI filter at all, there's nothing to move yet.

How to delete a Meta AI chat

WABetaInfo says you can delete one of the new AI chats whenever you want. For the current setup, WhatsApp's help page on deleting chats with Meta AI gives these steps.

On WhatsApp Web, Windows or Mac:

  1. Open the chat with Meta AI.
  2. Hover over the chat you want to remove and click the down arrow.
  3. Click Delete, then Delete again.

To remove several at once, click the three dots, choose Select chats, tick the ones you want, then click the trash can and Delete.

On Android or iPhone (as quoted from the same page by Kanal on October 7, 2026):

  1. Open the chat with Meta AI.
  2. Tap the chat history icon (a clock with a circular arrow).
  3. Press and hold the chat you want to delete.
  4. Tap Delete chat, then Delete.

WhatsApp warns that a deleted Meta AI chat can't be restored. If there's an answer you want to keep, copy it somewhere first.

Deleting the chat doesn't delete Meta's copy

Deleting a Meta AI chat removes it from your device. Meta AI keeps its own copy of what you sent, and WhatsApp says you need a command to remove that:

  • One chat: open the chat with Meta AI, select the chat you want to reset, type /reset-ai in the message box and send it.
  • Everything: open the chat with Meta AI, type /reset-all-ais and send it. This resets every individual and group chat where you've talked to Meta AI.

If you're cleaning up, send the command first and delete the chat after, so you're not hunting for a chat you already removed.

WhatsApp's About Meta AI page also says Meta may use your prompts, the messages you share and your feedback to improve its AI. Treat a Meta AI chat like any other online service and don't paste in passwords, ID numbers or other people's private messages.

Can you turn Meta AI off completely?

Not with any setting WhatsApp documents. Kanal searched WhatsApp's Help Center on October 7, 2026 and found no switch that removes Meta AI from the app. What you can control:

  • In one chat: turn on Restricted chat from the chat info screen. WhatsApp's help page says people in that chat then can't use Meta AI features there, can't export the chat, can't auto-save media and can't open the chat on linked devices. In groups only admins can turn it on; in a one-to-one chat either person can. That last limit matters if you use WhatsApp Web, since the restricted chat won't be usable there.
  • In a group: if someone added Meta AI as a group member, an admin can remove it from the group's member list. WhatsApp notes this feature is only available on certain devices.
  • Your own use: if you never open the Meta AI chat or mention @Meta AI, nothing is sent to it. WhatsApp's About Meta AI page says Meta can only read messages that mention @Meta AI or that people choose to share with it, and your other personal messages stay end-to-end encrypted.
