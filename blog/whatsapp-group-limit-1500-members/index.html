WhatsApp group limit: 1,024 members, and the 1,500 rollout report

Published November 19, 2026

The WhatsApp group limit is 1,024 members according to WhatsApp's own Help Center, and on November 19, 2026 the feature tracker WABetaInfo reported that a 1,500-member limit is rolling out to some Android and iPhone users.

WhatsApp itself hasn't announced the change. Its Can't join group page still says "A WhatsApp group can have up to 1,024 members", and so does its page on adding and removing group members. The 1,500 figure comes from WABetaInfo's report, which Beebom repeated the same day. Treat it as a report until WhatsApp's pages change.

If you run a big school, building, club or customer group, here is what the report says and what to do when your group still won't take one more person.

What the 1,500-member report says

According to WABetaInfo:

  • The maximum group size is going from 1,024 to 1,500 members, on Android and iPhone.
  • Existing groups get the new limit automatically. Admins don't need to change a setting or start a new group.
  • A group that is already full at 1,024 can take up to 476 more people.
  • Groups inside a Community get the same 1,500 limit.
  • The Community announcement group is not part of this change. WhatsApp's community announcements page already says you can add up to 2,000 members to a community and its announcement group.

The rollout is gradual. WABetaInfo says that if you can't add more than 1,024 members yet, the update should reach more accounts over the coming weeks, and WhatsApp hasn't said when everyone will have it.

How to tell if your group has the new limit

There's no switch to look for. The test is whether you can add member number 1,025.

  1. Update WhatsApp from the Play Store or App Store.
  2. Open the group, tap the group name, and check the member count in Group info.
  3. If you're at 1,024 and you're an admin, try adding one more person.

If WhatsApp still stops you at 1,024, your account doesn't have the new limit yet.

WABetaInfo's report covers Android and iPhone. It doesn't mention WhatsApp Web or the desktop app. If you manage the group from a computer and get stopped at 1,024, try adding the person from your updated phone.

WhatsApp's Help Center pages still say 1,024 as of November 19, 2026. If your group takes member number 1,025, the new limit has reached you even though the Help Center hasn't caught up.

"Can't join group" and the group isn't full

A full group is only one reason a link fails. WhatsApp's Can't join group page lists four:

  • Approve new members is on. You have to request to join, and an admin has to accept you.
  • The group is at its size limit.
  • The invite link was reset. The old link is dead. Ask an admin for a new link or to add you directly.
  • An internal server error. Try again later.

Check the first and third before you assume the group is full. If your link came from an old forwarded message, ask an admin whether it was reset.

If the group is inside a Community, WhatsApp's page on joining community groups says you can request to join a full group, and you're added when a member leaves or is removed.

If you're the admin and the group is full

Until the 1,500 limit reaches your account, room opens only when someone leaves or an admin removes them. To remove someone on WhatsApp Web, the Help Center's steps are:

  1. Open the group chat and click the group name at the top.
  2. In Group info, hover over the member you want to remove and click the arrow next to their name.
  3. Click Remove, then Remove again to confirm.

The same page warns that if you add too many members to groups, you may see an error and have to wait 24 hours before adding more or creating new groups. If you're about to add a few hundred people once the new limit arrives, spread it out.

If you want control over who fills the new seats, turn on Approve new members in Group permissions. WhatsApp's admin approval page says it's off by default, and when it's on you'll see a Review prompt in the chat for pending requests.

Should you merge your overflow groups?

Beebom notes that many large communities had to split their members across several groups to get past 1,024. Once the 1,500 limit reaches you, two groups that together have fewer than 1,500 people could fit in one.

Before you merge, think about what a bigger group does to the chat:

  • More strangers see you. WhatsApp's add-members page says a new member's profile name may be visible to everyone else in the group.
  • More messages. A group that busy is hard to follow. WhatsApp's newer tools help here: pinning a message for longer, the Mentions and replies filter, and sending message history to new members so people joining from the old group can catch up.
  • Announcements might fit better elsewhere. If most members only need updates from the admins, a Community announcement group (up to 2,000 members) keeps the chat quiet.

If you're a member and a 1,500-person group is too much, you can mute it, or read our guide to finding the important messages buried in a WhatsApp group.

FAQ

What is the WhatsApp group limit right now?

WhatsApp's Help Center says a group can have up to 1,024 members. On November 19, 2026 WABetaInfo reported that a 1,500-member limit is rolling out to some Android and iPhone users, but WhatsApp hasn't announced it.

How do I know if my group can have 1,500 members?

There's no setting to check. Update WhatsApp on your phone, and if your group is at 1,024 and you're an admin, try adding one more person. If WhatsApp stops you, your account doesn't have the higher limit yet.

Do existing groups need to be recreated to get the higher limit?

No. According to WABetaInfo, existing groups get the new limit automatically once the update reaches the admin's account.

Does the change affect Community announcement groups?

No. WABetaInfo says the announcement group isn't part of this change, and WhatsApp's Help Center already says you can add up to 2,000 members to a community and its announcement group.

Why can't I join a WhatsApp group that isn't full?

WhatsApp's Help Center lists other reasons: an admin turned on Approve new members, the invite link was reset, or there was a server error. Ask an admin for a new link or to add you directly.
