How to mute a WhatsApp chat forever (Always) on Android, iPhone and WhatsApp Web

Published November 5, 2026

WhatsApp lets you mute a chat for 8 hours, 1 week, or Always. Always means the chat stays silent until you unmute it yourself. Messages still arrive, and the unread count still shows next to the chat. The other person is not told that you muted them.

WhatsApp's help pages cover the same three durations on Android, iPhone and WhatsApp Web. Below are the steps for each, plus what mute does and does not change.

Mute on Android

Individual chat

  1. Open the chat.
  2. Tap the name of the chat.
  3. Tap Notifications, then turn on Mute.
  4. Pick 8 hours, 1 week, or Always, then tap OK.

Group chat

  1. Open the group.
  2. Tap the name of the group.
  3. Tap Notifications.
  4. Turn on Mute under Messages or Calls, depending on which alerts you want to stop.
  5. Pick a duration, then tap OK.

On groups, messages and calls have separate mute switches, so you can silence group texts and still get call alerts, or the other way around.

Mute on iPhone

Individual chat

  1. Open the chat.
  2. Tap the name of the person.
  3. Tap Notifications > Mute notification, then pick the length of time.

Group chat

  1. Open the group.
  2. Tap the name of the group.
  3. Tap Notifications.
  4. Tap Mute notifications under Messages or Calls.
  5. Pick the length of time.

To unmute on iPhone, open a muted chat, tap Muted, then Unmute.

Mute on WhatsApp Web

  1. Open the individual or group chat.
  2. Click the name of the chat at the top.
  3. Turn on Mute notifications.
  4. Pick the length of time, then click Mute.

To unmute on Web, open the muted chat, turn off Muted, then click Unmute.

One sync detail from WhatsApp's Web help page: if you mute a group on your phone, that group is also muted on WhatsApp Web and Desktop. Other notification settings stay independent between your phone and your computer, so changing message sounds on Web does not change them on the phone.

What mute does and does not do

According to WhatsApp's mute help pages:

  • Your device will not vibrate or make noise for that chat.
  • Messages still arrive in the chat.
  • Contacts are not notified that you muted them.
  • If you have read receipts on, contacts still get a read receipt when you open and read their messages.
  • The unread message count still appears next to the chat in your list.

The chat stays in your list. You still see new messages when you open WhatsApp and look at that row. You just stop getting a sound or vibration for that one chat.

Mute a list instead of one chat

On Android and iPhone you can also mute a whole list (the contact lists you create in WhatsApp):

  1. Open Settings (or your profile picture) > Lists.
  2. Tap the list you want to mute.
  3. Turn on Mute and pick 8 hours, 1 week, or Always.

WhatsApp notes that contacts still will not see that you muted them, and you will still get a notification if someone in that list mentions you. So a list mute is quieter than leaving every chat alone, but it is not a full ignore of @mentions.

Device settings can override WhatsApp mute

If WhatsApp as a whole is silent on your phone, muting or unmuting one chat inside the app will not bring the sound back. WhatsApp's notifications help page says device settings override in-app settings. If you silenced WhatsApp under Android or iPhone notification settings, fix that first, then set per-chat mute inside WhatsApp.

On WhatsApp Web, closing WhatsApp on your computer stops desktop notifications without changing your mute settings. WhatsApp's notifications help page says you will not get notifications while WhatsApp stays closed.

Quick FAQ

Does Always expire? No. Always stays on until you unmute that chat.

Can the other person tell I muted them? No. WhatsApp says contacts are not notified.

Will I still see unread badges? Yes. The unread count still shows next to the chat.

Do read receipts stop when I mute? No. If you have read receipts enabled, they still send when you read the messages.

I muted a group on my phone. Why is it muted on Web too? WhatsApp syncs group mute from phone to WhatsApp Web and Desktop. Other notification settings do not sync that way.
