Changing your WhatsApp number? Keep your chats, and a copy

Published November 28, 2026

To change your WhatsApp number without losing your chats, use Change number in WhatsApp on your phone (Settings > Account > Change number) while the old number still works: WhatsApp moves your chats, groups and settings to the new number, but backups made with the old number can't be restored afterwards, so make a new backup straight away.

The steps and rules below are from WhatsApp's Help Center pages, read on November 28, 2026. At the end is what our extension, WA Folders, put in an offline copy of a chat when we ran its export code the same day.

What Change number moves, and what it doesn't

According to WhatsApp's About changing phone numbers page, Change number moves your profile photo, username, display name, privacy settings, WhatsApp contacts, individual chats and group chats to the new number, and deletes the account tied to the old one, so contacts stop seeing the old number.

  • Backups don't follow you. Cloud backups are linked to the number used when they were made. After the change, a backup made with the old number can no longer restore your data. WhatsApp "highly" recommends a new backup right after the change.
  • Groups are always told. Your groups are notified of the change even if you leave Notify contacts off. For your other contacts you choose: all contacts, contacts you have chats with, or a custom list.
  • Blocked contacts stay blocked and aren't told your new number.
  • There's no undo. If you change by mistake and go back to the old number, a group admin has to add you to each group again.
  • Broadcast lists only reach people who saved the new number in their phone's contacts.

If your old number is about to expire

Do the change before the old SIM stops working. WhatsApp's Can't change phone number page says the old number has to be the one currently registered, and that if you lose access to your old SIM or number without using Change number, you permanently lose access to that WhatsApp account and have to create a new one.

The chats themselves are stored on your phone. WhatsApp's inactive account deletion page says accounts are generally deleted after 365 days without connecting, and that content stored on the device stays there until WhatsApp is deleted from it. That is a reason not to delete the app in a panic, not a plan: the safe route is still Change number while the old SIM works.

How to change your number on the same phone

  1. Put the new SIM in the phone. The new number must be able to get an SMS or a call. VoIP, toll-free, premium-rate and private numbers aren't accepted, and landlines only work on WhatsApp Business.
  2. Android: tap the three-dot menu, then Settings > Account > Change number > Next. iPhone: open Settings (or your profile picture), then Account > Change Number > Next.
  3. Enter the old number in the first field and the new one in the second, both in full international format, and tap Next.
  4. Choose whether to notify contacts, tap Done, and register the new number with the code WhatsApp sends.
  5. Make a new backup (Google Account on Android, iCloud on iPhone).

You can't do any of this from WhatsApp Web or WhatsApp Desktop. WhatsApp's How to change your phone number page says it has to be done on your primary phone, and the option is greyed out on a linked device.

New number and a new phone

Change the number on the old phone first, then make a backup there with the new number. On the new phone, install WhatsApp, register the new number and restore that backup. A backup only restores with the number it was made with, which is why the order matters.

Keep your own copy of the chats that matter

A backup lives in WhatsApp's own restore system. If you also want a file you can open anywhere, for an order, a landlord or a work client, save the important chats before you switch. On a computer, WhatsApp Web's own Export chat (More options > Export chat) saves the text as a ZIP file, without media; see how to export a WhatsApp Web chat for what each method includes.

We build WA Folders, a Chrome extension for WhatsApp Web, so on November 28, 2026 we ran its export code (version 2.5.0, the version on the Chrome Web Store that day) on a four-message chat we made up: a phone-shop agent confirming the new SIM, plus a note we attached. No real chat was used. Here is what came out:

  • HTML (1,912 bytes): a page with the chat name, "Exported by WA Folders on 2026-11-28 · 4 messages", our note in a yellow box, then the bubbles with sender and time. It opens in any browser, offline.
  • Markdown (459 bytes): the same, as a heading, the note as a quote and one list item per message.
  • CSV (374 bytes): four columns, direction, time, sender, text, with a byte-order mark so Excel shows the emoji correctly. A message that started with =HYPERLINK( was saved with a leading apostrophe, so a spreadsheet shows it as text instead of running it. With the browser set to Brazilian Portuguese, the separator switched to a semicolon.
  • The file was named WhatsApp - Phone shop (Ana) - 2026-11-28.md, so copies from different days don't overwrite each other.

FAQ

Will I lose my chats if I change my WhatsApp number?

Not if you use Change number in WhatsApp on your phone. WhatsApp says it moves your individual chats, group chats, contacts, profile and settings to the new number. Make a new backup afterwards, because backups made with the old number can't restore your data any more.

What happens if my old number expires before I change it?

WhatsApp says that if you lose access to your old SIM or number without using Change number, you permanently lose access to that account and have to create a new one. Chats stored on the phone stay there until WhatsApp is deleted from it, but you can't move them to a new account that way. Change the number while the old SIM still works.

Can I change my WhatsApp number on WhatsApp Web?

No. WhatsApp says the number can only be changed on your primary phone; the option is greyed out on linked devices such as WhatsApp Web and WhatsApp Desktop.

Do my contacts find out I changed my number?

Your groups are always notified. For your other contacts you choose whether to notify all of them, only people you have chats with, or a custom list. Blocked contacts are not told.

How do I keep a copy of a WhatsApp chat before switching numbers?

On a computer, WhatsApp Web's Export chat saves the text as a ZIP file, without media. WA Folders, a Chrome extension, exports the open chat as HTML, CSV, Markdown or JSON; the free plan allows 10 exports a day of the messages loaded on screen.
