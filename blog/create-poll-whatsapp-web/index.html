How to create a poll on WhatsApp Web (and see who voted)

Published November 27, 2026 · Em português

To make a poll on WhatsApp Web, open the group or chat, click the attach button next to the message box and choose Poll. Type a question of up to 255 characters and up to 12 options of up to 100 characters each, decide whether people can pick more than one answer, and click Send. To see the results, click View votes under the poll (WhatsApp Help Center).

The steps on WhatsApp Web

These follow WhatsApp's Help Center page "How to use polls" for Web, checked November 27, 2026 (WhatsApp Help Center).

  1. Open the chat or group where you want to ask.
  2. Click the attach button to the left of the message box (a plus sign on current versions), then click Poll.
  3. Under Question, type what you want to ask. The limit is 255 characters.
  4. Under Options, add up to 12 answers of up to 100 characters each. Drag the handle next to an option to move it up or down.
  5. Leave Allow multiple answers on if people may pick several options. Turn it off to give each person one vote.
  6. Click Send.

Multiple answers is on by default, so check that toggle before you send a "pick one date" poll.

Voting, changing a vote and seeing the results

  • Vote: click the option, or options, you want.
  • Take a vote back: click the same option again.
  • Change your answer in a one-vote poll: click a different option. WhatsApp removes your earlier pick.
  • See the results: open the chat, find the poll and click View votes. You see the count for each option and how many people have voted.
  • Notifications: if you made the poll, WhatsApp sends one push notification per poll when votes come in, as long as WhatsApp notifications are on (WhatsApp Help Center). Our guide to WhatsApp Web notifications that don't show up covers the browser side.

The Help Center doesn't describe a list of people who haven't voted. In a small group you can compare the names in View votes with the member list in the group info panel.

Edit or delete a poll

  • Fix the question: you have 15 minutes after sending. Click the poll, click Edit, change the question and send it again. The Help Center only mentions editing the question, not the options.
  • Delete it: click the small arrow in the top right corner of the poll, click Delete, then choose Delete for everyone or Delete for me.

What the phone app has that WhatsApp Web doesn't (yet)

WhatsApp's Android page for polls lists two more settings: Hide voter names, which shows vote counts without names to everyone including you, and Set an end time, which closes voting at the time you pick. The same page says poll settings can't be changed after you create the poll; to use different ones, delete it and make a new one. Android also has a Polls filter in chat search (WhatsApp Help Center, Android). The Web version of the page doesn't mention any of these, as of November 27, 2026.

So on a computer, put the deadline in the question itself, such as "Which Saturday works? Vote by Friday 6 pm". If you need anonymous votes or an automatic close, create the poll on your phone.

If Poll is missing or a poll won't send

WhatsApp's own troubleshooting list for Web is short: restart the computer, update the browser, and check that other websites load (WhatsApp Help Center). If WhatsApp Web itself keeps spinning, see WhatsApp Web stuck loading chats.

Polls in a channel you run are a separate feature: they are public, can use photos, can't be edited and can be deleted for up to 30 days (WhatsApp Help Center, channel polls).

Keep the groups you poll in one place
