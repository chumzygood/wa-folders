How to block someone on WhatsApp Web, and what they can still see

Published November 16, 2026

You can block a contact or an unknown number from WhatsApp Web or the desktop app without touching your phone. The person isn't told. After that, their messages, calls and status updates stop reaching you, and they stop seeing your last seen, online status, status updates and new profile photos (WhatsApp Help Center).

A block is narrower than most people expect, though. Groups, your contact list and the old chat all carry on as before. The details are below.

Block someone on WhatsApp Web or the desktop app

  1. In the chat list, hover over the chat with the person and open its menu. You can also open the chat and use the menu at the top right of the chat window.
  2. Click Block, then Block again to confirm.
  3. Before you confirm, check the box next to Report to WhatsApp only if you also want to report them. Reporting sends WhatsApp up to five of the last messages they sent you, and they aren't told (How to block and report someone).

The same steps work for a business or a number that isn't in your contacts. On Android, open the chat, tap the menu, then More > Block. WhatsApp keeps the phone steps in its own articles (Android steps, iPhone steps).

What changes, and what doesn't

  • They aren't notified. Only you see "You blocked this person", under their name and in your chat with them (WhatsApp Help Center).
  • Your old chat stays. The conversation remains in your chat list. You can delete it, or archive it to move it out of sight. See how archiving works.
  • Contacts stay. Blocking doesn't remove them from your phone's contacts, and it doesn't remove you from theirs. To delete a contact, you delete it from your phone's address book.
  • Groups are not covered. If you're both in a group, they still see your messages there and you still see theirs. You can't block a group itself; you can exit it and block the admin (WhatsApp Help Center). Our guide to leaving a group quietly covers what members see when you go.
  • Online in groups. If your online setting is Everyone, you can still show as online in a group they're in. They can't see that you're online specifically.
  • Status timing. A status you posted before blocking them stays visible to them until it expires. Anything you post after the block, they won't see.
  • New numbers and new accounts. If they write from another number, you have to block that number too. If you set up a new WhatsApp account yourself, your blocks don't carry over and you need to add them again.

What the blocked person notices

WhatsApp doesn't tell anyone they've been blocked and won't confirm it if they ask. From their side, a block looks like this (Being blocked by someone):

  • Messages to you show one gray check mark and never get a second one.
  • Calls to you don't go through.
  • They can't see your last seen, online status, status updates or new profile photos.
  • If they're a group admin, they can't add you to a group.

WhatsApp points out that each of these signs can have other causes, such as a phone that's switched off. We go through those in why a WhatsApp message shows one gray tick.

See and edit your blocked list

Your blocked contacts are listed under Settings > Privacy > Blocked contacts (WhatsApp Help Center). To unblock someone from WhatsApp Web, open the menu on their chat, or the menu at the top right of the chat window, then click Unblock and Unblock again.

Unblocking doesn't bring anything back. Messages, calls and status updates they sent while blocked are not delivered to you afterwards (WhatsApp Help Center).

If you're blocking because of harassment

Keep a record before you delete anything. WhatsApp suggests screenshots of the relevant messages, calls or profile details, or a backup of your chat history, and says to contact local law enforcement if you feel unsafe or think a crime has been committed (WhatsApp Help Center). On WhatsApp Web you can also export the chat: open it, open the menu at the top, click Export chat and choose all messages or a date range (How to export your chat history). Our export guide explains what the file contains.

If the trouble is calls from strangers rather than one person, Silence unknown callers may be enough on its own.
