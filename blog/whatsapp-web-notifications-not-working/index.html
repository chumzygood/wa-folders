WhatsApp Web notifications not working in Chrome? 7 things to check

Published November 11, 2026

WhatsApp Web can only show a pop-up if four things agree: WhatsApp Web's own settings, Chrome's permission for web.whatsapp.com, your computer's notification settings, and the chat itself not being muted. When messages arrive silently, one of those four is usually switched off. Work through the list below in order, starting with the two Chrome checks.

1. Click Turn on in the banner above your chats

When notifications are off, WhatsApp Web shows a banner above the chat list asking you to turn on desktop notifications. WhatsApp's Help Center says to click Turn on in that banner and follow the prompts. Chrome then asks whether web.whatsapp.com may show notifications. Click Allow.

No banner? Refresh the page. If it still doesn't appear, WhatsApp says you may have muted or turned off notifications in WhatsApp's own settings (step 3).

2. Unblock web.whatsapp.com in Chrome

If you once clicked Block on that prompt, Chrome remembers it. To undo it, follow WhatsApp's steps:

  1. In Chrome, click the three-dot menu > Settings.
  2. Go to Privacy and security > Site settings > Notifications.
  3. Make sure Sites can ask to send notifications is on.
  4. If https://web.whatsapp.com is in the Not allowed to send notifications list, click the three dots next to it and choose Allow.

A quicker route from Chrome Help: with WhatsApp Web open, click the icon to the left of the address, then turn on Notifications.

Chrome can also take the permission away on its own. Google says Chrome may remove notification permission from sites you haven't visited in a while, and it shows a "Permissions removed" note in Safety Check when it does. If WhatsApp Web notifications stopped after a few weeks away from the computer, this is a likely reason. Allow the site again and they come back.

3. Check WhatsApp Web's own notification settings

Inside WhatsApp Web, open Settings > Notifications. The Help Center lists these boxes:

  • Message notifications: the pop-up for new messages. This is the main one.
  • Show previews: shows the message text inside the pop-up. Turn it off if people can see your screen.
  • Show reaction notifications: alerts when someone reacts to a message you sent.
  • Background sync: syncs messages in the background for faster performance.
  • Incoming sounds and Outgoing sounds: the sound alerts.

If pop-ups work but there's no sound, Incoming sounds is the box to check.

4. Keep WhatsApp Web open

WhatsApp's Help Center says that if WhatsApp is closed on your computer, you won't get notifications there. For WhatsApp Web that means the tab has to stay open in a running Chrome window. Close the tab, or quit Chrome, and the alerts stop until you open it again. Keeping the tab pinned (right-click the tab > Pin) makes it harder to close by mistake.

Incognito windows don't help here. Chrome Help says you won't get notifications while browsing privately.

5. Turn off Do Not Disturb on Windows or Mac

Chrome can allow WhatsApp Web and the computer can still hide the pop-up.

Windows 11. Go to Start > Settings > System > Notifications. Check that notifications are on and that Google Chrome is on in the list of apps. Then look at Do not disturb. Microsoft says it sends notifications to the notification center instead of showing banners, and it can switch itself on automatically at set times, while you share or duplicate your screen, while you play a game, in full-screen apps, and for the first hour after a Windows feature update. Expand Turn on do not disturb automatically to see which of those are ticked. You can also add Chrome under priority notifications so it gets through.

Mac. Open System Settings > Notifications. Under Application Notifications, click Google Chrome and turn on Allow notifications (Apple's steps). If you also see Google Chrome Helper (Alerts) in that list, turn it on too. Then check that a Focus such as Do Not Disturb isn't on.

6. Check whether the chat is muted

A muted chat gets no pop-up or sound. WhatsApp says the messages still arrive and the unread count still shows next to the chat. On WhatsApp Web, open the chat, click its name at the top to open the chat info, and check the mute setting there.

Groups have an extra rule. WhatsApp's Help Center says that when you mute a group's messages you'll still get notifications for mentions and replies to you. In the phone app, a group's Highlights setting limits its alerts to mentions, replies, messages from contacts and other messages WhatsApp treats as relevant. Our guide on muting a chat forever covers the options, and the Mentions and replies filter shows how to find the messages where you were tagged.

7. Reload, then link the computer again

If everything above is on and nothing arrives, reload WhatsApp Web with Ctrl+R (Cmd+R on Mac). If it still stays quiet, log out of WhatsApp Web from its menu and link it again from your phone under Linked devices. If the QR code gives you trouble, you can link with your phone number instead. If WhatsApp Web keeps signing you out on its own, that's a separate problem with its own fixes: why WhatsApp Web keeps logging you out.

The other problem: too many notifications

Some people have the opposite trouble. Someone on r/CommunityManager who joined about 10 WhatsApp communities in two weeks wrote that valuable messages "are buried under noise" and that "I muted all my new groups within days." If that's you, mute the busy groups and keep alerts for mentions and replies, which WhatsApp still sends for muted groups. That way you stay reachable when someone tags you without getting a pop-up for every message.
