How to pin a WhatsApp message forever, and why your old pins disappeared

Published October 30, 2026

Until this week, every pinned message on WhatsApp had an expiry date. You picked 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, and when the time ran out the pin quietly dropped off the top of the chat. That's why the group rules or the address you pinned a month ago are no longer there.

On October 29, 2026, WhatsApp announced that you can now keep a message pinned forever if you don't want to pick a time limit. It's one of four group chat updates in the same announcement, and it's rolling out gradually, so you may not have it yet. Another of the four is a filter for group chats where you were tagged or replied to, covered in WhatsApp's Mentions and replies filter.

What changed

The old duration choices still exist. The new part is a no-limit option, so a pin stays until someone unpins it. WhatsApp's blog post describes it as keeping "important messages pinned forever".

WABetaInfo, which tracks WhatsApp releases, reports that the update is reaching some users on the latest Android and iPhone versions first, with more accounts getting it over the coming weeks. If you don't see the option, update WhatsApp from your app store and check again in a few days. There's no setting you can switch on to get it sooner.

Pin a message on WhatsApp Web or Desktop

  1. Open the chat and hover over the message you want to pin.
  2. Click the down arrow that appears on the message.
  3. Click Pin.
  4. Pick how long it should stay pinned. If your account has the update, the list includes the option with no time limit.
  5. Click Pin to confirm.

The newest pin shows as a banner at the top of the chat. Click the banner to jump to the original message.

Pin a message on Android or iPhone

  1. Open the chat and press and hold the message.
  2. On Android, tap the three-dot menu, then Pin. On iPhone, tap More, then Pin.
  3. Choose the duration and confirm.

WhatsApp's feature page says you can keep up to three messages pinned in a chat. If you've hit the limit, unpin one first. Some news reports this week mention a higher limit, so if WhatsApp lets you pin a fourth, your account has the newer limit.

Unpin a message

On WhatsApp Web, hover over the pinned message (in the chat or on the banner), click the down arrow and choose Unpin. On a phone, press and hold the message and tap Unpin. A forever pin only goes away this way, so if a pinned message is out of date, someone has to remove it by hand.

Who can pin in a group

In a group, the admins decide. If you don't see Pin on a message, the group is probably set so only admins can change group settings, and pinning falls under that setting.

To change it as an admin on WhatsApp Web, click the group name at the top of the chat, open Group permissions, and turn Edit group settings on to let everyone pin, or off to keep it to admins. The same switch also controls who can change the group name, icon and description, so turning it on hands out all of those at once. On a phone the path is the same: tap the group name, then Group permissions.

Whenever anyone pins a message in a group, WhatsApp posts a system message that says who pinned it. You can't pin silently.

Why some people can't see the pin

This catches group admins out. A member who joined after the message was sent won't see it pinned, because the original message never reached their phone. The same goes for someone who cleared or lost their chat history, or who deleted that message before it was pinned.

The second update in the same announcement helps here. Earlier this year WhatsApp added group message history, which lets you send between 25 and 100 recent messages to a person when you add them. Now you can also send that history after someone has joined, by tapping their name in the chat. If the pinned message is among the recent messages you send, the new member at least gets the message itself. Admins can turn off members' ability to send history in group permissions, but admins can always send it.

For rules a group needs to see permanently, the group description is still worth using alongside a pin. Back when WhatsApp had no pins at all, that was the standard advice on r/whatsapp, and it still reaches everyone, including people who joined yesterday.

Pin, star or note?

These three get mixed up, so here's the difference:

  • Pin puts a message at the top of the chat for everyone in it.
  • Star saves a message to your own Starred messages list. Nobody else sees it. Our post on finding starred messages covers where that list lives.
  • Pin a chat is a different feature. It keeps a whole conversation at the top of your chat list, and only you see it. See how to pin more than three chats on WhatsApp Web if you've run out of chat pins.

If the message matters to the whole group, pin it. If it only matters to you, star it.
