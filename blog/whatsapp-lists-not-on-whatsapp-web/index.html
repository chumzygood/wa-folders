WhatsApp Lists aren't available on WhatsApp Web

Published November 13, 2026

WhatsApp Lists are not available on WhatsApp Web. WhatsApp's How to use lists page, opened with the Web tab selected and re-read on 13 November 2026, says: "This feature isn't available on the web app, but it is available on these devices." The devices it names are Android and iPhone.

If you built Work or Family lists on your phone and then opened web.whatsapp.com looking for the same groups, that is why they are not there. The Help Center is describing a phone feature.

What Lists are, on the phone

WhatsApp's About lists page, also re-read today, calls Lists "customizable filters that can help you keep your chats organized." You find them by going to the Chats tab and swiping down.

Every account starts with four lists:

  • All: every chat.
  • Unread: chats you have not opened, or that you marked unread.
  • Favorites: chats from contacts you marked as favorites.
  • Groups: all your group chats.

You can add your own lists, for example Work or Family, and put people or groups in them. The Android how-to says you need at least one chat in a list to create it, and you can add or remove chats later. A selected list stays on until you pick a different one, or you leave WhatsApp and open it again. The default All, Unread and Groups lists cannot be reordered by dragging chats around inside them.

Lists are a filter. They do not move the underlying chats, and they are not the same thing as pinning a chat or archiving it.

Why this shows up as a desktop problem

People who work from WhatsApp Web often want the same Work or Family grouping they already set on the phone. A recent Reddit thread about WhatsApp Web's lists or labels put it this way: the writer now has to pick up the phone to edit labels they used to handle on the computer.

That is a user report, not a WhatsApp Help Center statement. What WhatsApp itself says, for a regular account, is simpler: Lists are documented for Android and iPhone, and the Web tab of the same article says the feature is not on the web app.

Your WhatsApp Web window may still show a row of filters above the chat list, such as Unread or Groups. Which of those appear depends on your account, so look at your own screen. Those filters are not the custom Lists you created on the phone.

What you can do on WhatsApp Web instead

On the computer, WhatsApp's own tools are the filters that do appear, pinning more than three chats, and keeping work and personal chats in separate groups of your own. If you want the same grouping on your phone and your computer, keep using Lists on the phone. They will not appear on WhatsApp Web, per the Help Center.

If you want folders you can click on web.whatsapp.com, that is a different tool, on the computer only.

FAQ

Are WhatsApp Lists on WhatsApp Web?

No. WhatsApp's How to use lists page, with the Web tab selected, says the feature is not available on the web app. It is available on Android and iPhone.

Where do I find Lists on my phone?

Open the Chats tab and swipe down, according to WhatsApp's About lists page. The same article describes four default lists (All, Unread, Favorites, Groups) and custom lists you create yourself.

Why don't the Work or Family lists from my phone appear on my computer?

Because Lists are a phone feature. The Web help page says they are not on the web app, so WhatsApp Web has no place to show the custom lists you made on Android or iPhone.

Can I edit those phone lists from WhatsApp Web?

WhatsApp does not provide Lists on the web app, so there is no official Web screen for creating or editing them. Edit them on your phone, from the Chats tab or from Settings, then Lists, as the Android how-to describes.

Do WA Folders folders show up on my phone?

No. They only appear in Chrome, on WhatsApp Web, and they stay on that computer. They do not sync to the WhatsApp app.
