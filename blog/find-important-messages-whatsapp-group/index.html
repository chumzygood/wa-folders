How to find important messages buried in a WhatsApp group

Published October 30, 2026

To find an important message buried in a WhatsApp group, open the group and search it for a word you remember from the message, or jump to the day it was sent with search by date. If the message tagged you or replied to you, the @ marker and WhatsApp's new Mentions and replies filter get you there faster.

A group with 90 unread messages usually holds one or two that matter: a date, a payment, a change of plan. Scrolling back for them is slow and easy to get wrong. Here is what to try, quickest first.

1. Look for the @ before you open the group

In your chats list, a small @ next to an unread group means someone mentioned you or replied to one of your messages there. The WhatsApp Help Center says you'll see those messages when you open the chat. Open the group from the list while the @ is still showing, because the marker is tied to the unread messages.

A mention also gets through when you've muted the group. According to the Help Center, tagging someone overrides their mute settings for that group, unless they've also muted @all mentions.

2. Use the Mentions and replies filter

On October 29, 2026, WhatsApp announced a new chat filter that shows every unread group conversation where you were mentioned or got a direct reply, including @all messages. It sits with the other filters at the top of the chats list. WhatsApp says the update is meant to help you catch up on the chats most relevant to you.

It's new, so you may not have it yet. Update WhatsApp and check the filter bar again in a few days. For more on how it works, see how to find where you were mentioned in a WhatsApp group.

3. Search inside the group

When the message didn't mention you, search is the fastest route. On WhatsApp Web:

  1. Open the group.
  2. Click the search icon at the top of the chat.
  3. Type a word or phrase you remember, such as "deposit", "Saturday" or the person's name.
  4. Click a result to jump to that message in the chat.

Pick an unusual word. "Meeting" will match dozens of messages, while "venue" or a street name might match one. You can also search from the Chats tab to look across every chat at once. WhatsApp Web is a linked device and may not hold your full history, so if an older message doesn't turn up, search the same group on your phone.

4. Jump to the day it was sent

If you remember roughly when it arrived but not the words, WhatsApp can search a chat by date. Open the group, click the search icon, then the calendar icon, and pick the date. The chat jumps to that day and you can read forward from there. The guide to searching WhatsApp Web by date has more detail.

5. Star it or pin it once you find it

Don't lose it a second time.

  • Star it if it matters only to you. Starred messages stay in one list you can open from the menu above your chat list. See where starred messages are on WhatsApp Web.
  • Pin it if the whole group needs it. A pin shows at the top of the chat for everyone, and WhatsApp's October 29 update added an option to keep a pin with no time limit. See how to pin a WhatsApp message forever.

If you run the group, an @all mention for the one message that really matters makes sure members see it, and the Mentions and replies filter now picks those up too. The Help Center says every member can use @all in groups of 32 or fewer, and only admins can in larger groups.

FAQ

How do I find an old message in a WhatsApp group without scrolling?

Open the group, click the search icon and type a word from the message, then click the result to jump to it. If you don't remember the words, use the calendar icon in the same search to jump to the date it was sent.

How do I see only the group messages that mention me?

Use the Mentions and replies filter in your chats list. WhatsApp announced it on October 29, 2026, and it shows every unread group chat where you were mentioned or got a direct reply, including @all messages. It's still rolling out, so update WhatsApp if you don't see it.

Does a mention reach me if I muted the group?

Yes, in most cases. The WhatsApp Help Center says a mention overrides your mute settings for that group, unless you've also muted @all mentions or muted the sender's individual chat.

How do I make sure an important message doesn't get buried again?

Star it to keep it in your own Starred messages list, or pin it so it stays at the top of the chat for everyone. Pins can now stay with no time limit.

Can I search every WhatsApp group at once?

Yes. Search from the Chats tab instead of inside a chat, and WhatsApp searches across your chats. On WhatsApp Web, WA Folders also searches the message text it has seen in chats you've opened and can save the search so you can rerun it.
