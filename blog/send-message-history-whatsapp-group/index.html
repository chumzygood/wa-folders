How to send message history to new WhatsApp group members (even after they join)

Published November 1, 2026

When someone joins a WhatsApp group, they see nothing that was said before they arrived. WhatsApp's Help Center says new members "won't be able to see events or messages sent in the group before they joined." People have been asking how to get around that for years; one r/whatsapp thread from 2023 got the answer "It will be brand new chat for new members."

That changed in February 2026, when WhatsApp added Group Message History. You can send a new member a batch of recent messages so they can catch up. On October 29 WhatsApp announced that you can also send it after the person has already joined, which helps if you forgot when you added them or they came in through an invite link.

What gets sent

  • Up to 100 recent messages. The February announcement describes a choice from 25 up to 100. The Help Center page on adding members says up to 100 messages from the last 14 days.
  • Pinned messages. You can include all of the group's pinned messages along with the recent ones.
  • A notice to everyone. The new member gets a notification, and the whole group sees a system message saying history was sent to them. WhatsApp says shared history looks different from regular messages and keeps the original senders and timestamps.

The messages stay end-to-end encrypted. Nothing is sent automatically; someone in the group has to choose to send it.

Send history when you add someone

Android

  1. Open the group and tap the group name.
  2. Tap Add.
  3. Pick the contacts or type a phone number.
  4. Check that Message history is on. If you want, choose how many recent messages to send.
  5. Tap Add members, then Continue.

iPhone

The steps match Android. After you choose the number of messages, tap Done, then Add members and Continue. You can also reach Group info by swiping the group left in the Chats tab and tapping More.

WhatsApp Web

  1. Open the group and click the group name, then Group info.
  2. Click Add member or Add participants.
  3. Check that Message history is on and pick how many messages to send.
  4. Click Add members.

Send history after someone has joined

If you skipped the toggle when you added them, WhatsApp's October 29 post says you can now tap the new member's name in the group chat and send recent messages from there. The Help Center also says you can find that member in the group and follow the same add steps to send history to someone already in the group.

If the person joined through an invite link, existing members see a Send message history option right in the chat. Tap it, choose how many messages if you want, and tap Send.

WhatsApp is rolling the October changes out gradually, so the option may not appear for everyone yet. Update WhatsApp first if you don't see it.

Why the option might be missing

You can only send history if you're allowed to add people. Admins can always add members and always send history. Regular members can add people only if the admins allow it, and even then an admin can switch off history sharing for members:

  1. Tap the group name.
  2. Tap Group permissions.
  3. Turn Send message history on or off.

If that switch is off, members won't see the history option when they add someone. Ask an admin to send it, or to turn the permission back on.

What message history leaves out

The Help Center lists what won't be included:

  • Messages that already disappeared because disappearing messages was on
  • Live locations
  • View once messages
  • Business transactions

If the new member reports content from the history you sent, you are the one reported, not the person who wrote the original message. Skim the last 100 messages before you share them. A trip-planning group can contain addresses, phone numbers or arguments that the newcomer doesn't need.

When 100 messages isn't enough

Message history covers recent messages only. If the new person needs the full background of a long-running group, such as months of decisions in a building committee, an export is the other option WhatsApp offers.

  • On your phone: open the group, tap More options > More > Export chat (Android), and choose Without media or Include media. You then send the file through the share sheet.
  • On WhatsApp Web: open the group, click More options > Export chat, choose All messages or Custom date range, and click Export. You get a ZIP file without media, and view once and disappearing messages are left out.

An export is a copy of the whole conversation, including everyone's names and numbers, so the same caution applies. It can't be re-imported into WhatsApp, so the new member reads it as a file rather than inside the group.

If the newcomer mainly needs a few key facts, a short pinned message posted after they join works too: the group's purpose, key dates and links. A pin on an older message won't show for someone who wasn't there when it was sent, as we covered in how to pin a WhatsApp message forever. If you're the new member and want to catch up on the parts that involve you, the Mentions and replies filter shows unread groups where someone tagged or replied to you.
