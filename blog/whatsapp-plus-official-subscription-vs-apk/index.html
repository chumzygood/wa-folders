Is WhatsApp Plus safe? The official subscription vs the APK

Published November 16, 2026

There are two things called WhatsApp Plus, and only one of them is safe. WhatsApp Plus the subscription is Meta's own paid add-on, bought inside the official WhatsApp app. WhatsApp Plus the APK is a modified copy of WhatsApp downloaded from a website, and WhatsApp says using apps like it can get your account temporarily or permanently banned.

If you were asked to pay inside WhatsApp, under Settings, that is the real subscription. If a site asked you to download a file to "get WhatsApp Plus free", that is the mod. Delete it.

The name came up again this week. On November 14, Infobae ran a warning about downloading the WhatsApp Plus APK, the mod that has used the same name for years.

What the official WhatsApp Plus subscription is

Meta announced its Meta One subscriptions in a newsroom post in September 2026. The WhatsApp-only plan, WhatsApp Plus, is listed at $2.99 a month. Meta says plans, benefits, pricing and availability vary by region, app and account, so the price you see may differ.

According to WhatsApp's own page and the Help Center article on Meta One plans, WhatsApp Plus includes:

  • Pinning up to 20 chats (the free app allows three).
  • Exclusive app themes and icons.
  • Stickers with special effects and exclusive ringtones.
  • Custom alerts, ringtones and themes for your chat lists.

Messages, calls and end-to-end encryption stay free whether you pay or not. Meta's post also says WhatsApp Plus will "soon test" storage for chat and media backups and Focus Schedules, so treat those as tests, not as features every subscriber gets today.

Where you can buy it

The Help Center says Meta One is bought through the Meta apps on iPhone or Android, is still in limited testing, and isn't available in every country. For WhatsApp users the plans work in WhatsApp Messenger, not the WhatsApp Business app.

iDownloadBlog's walkthrough gives the path:

  1. Update WhatsApp from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. On iPhone tap You; on Android tap the three dots, then Settings.
  3. Tap Subscriptions, then WhatsApp Plus.
  4. Confirm through Apple or Google billing.

If there's no Subscriptions entry, the plan probably isn't offered to your account yet. No website can sell it to you instead.

What it does on WhatsApp Web

You can't subscribe from WhatsApp Web, the desktop app or an iPad, according to iDownloadBlog. The same walkthrough says some benefits, such as chat themes and premium ringtones, don't show up on those devices even after you subscribe on your phone. If your main reason for paying is your computer, check what carries over before the first billing date.

To cancel, go back to Settings > Subscriptions > WhatsApp Plus, or cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

What the WhatsApp Plus APK is

The APK is a modified version of WhatsApp made by third parties, with extra settings added. You install it from a download site, and as an APK file it only runs on Android.

WhatsApp's page on unofficial apps doesn't name WhatsApp Plus in its current list of examples (it names GB WhatsApp, FM WhatsApp and others), but the rules it sets out cover any third-party version made without WhatsApp's approval:

  • Using one, or linking your account to one, breaks WhatsApp's Terms of Service.
  • WhatsApp says it can't check their security or privacy, and that they may carry malware that steals data.
  • Your account may be temporarily or permanently banned, or restricted, including losing the ability to link devices. That last one matters if you use WhatsApp Web every day.

Infobae adds that ignoring a temporary ban and staying on the unofficial app can turn it into a permanent one.

How to tell which one you have

  • Where did it come from? The official app comes from Google Play, the App Store or whatsapp.com. A file from any other website is unofficial.
  • Did you pay for it? The real subscription is billed through Apple or Google and shows up in your store's subscription list. The mod is usually advertised as free.
  • Is it on an iPhone? APK files are Android installers, so an iPhone can only have the subscription.
  • Are you seeing warnings? WhatsApp says users of unofficial apps may see "You need the official WhatsApp to log in" or "You have been logged out for using an unofficial app".

How to switch back to the official app

WhatsApp's steps on the unofficial apps page are short:

  1. Uninstall the unofficial app. WhatsApp asks you to remove all unofficial WhatsApp apps before registering again.
  2. Download WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business from Google Play or WhatsApp's website.
  3. Register your phone number again.

Be ready to lose some history. WhatsApp says only chats created in the official app can be backed up securely, and it can't promise that a backup made in an unofficial app will transfer.

After you're back on the official app, check Linked devices and log out any computer you don't recognise. Our post on malware and WhatsApp Web linked devices walks through it.

Do you need either one?

For most people the free app already covers messaging and calls. The subscription is mostly looks and more pins. If three pins is your actual problem, our post on pinning more than 3 chats on WhatsApp Web covers the free options first.

FAQ

Is WhatsApp Plus official?

The subscription is. Meta sells it inside WhatsApp Messenger for iPhone and Android. The WhatsApp Plus APK from download sites is not official and breaks WhatsApp's Terms of Service.

Can I get banned for WhatsApp Plus?

Not for the subscription. Using an unofficial app such as the WhatsApp Plus APK can lead to a temporary or permanent ban, according to WhatsApp's Help Center.

How much does WhatsApp Plus cost?

Meta lists it at $2.99 a month, with pricing that varies by region and account.

Can I buy WhatsApp Plus on WhatsApp Web?

No. You subscribe from the WhatsApp app on an iPhone or Android phone, and some benefits don't appear on WhatsApp Web or the desktop app.
