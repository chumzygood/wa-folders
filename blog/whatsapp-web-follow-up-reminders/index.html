WhatsApp reminders on WhatsApp Web: follow up when they don't reply

Published November 16, 2026 · Em português

To get reminded on WhatsApp Web when someone hasn't replied, set a follow-up reminder with a browser extension such as WA Folders: open the chat, choose Remind me if they don't reply in 1 or 3 days, and the chat comes back under Due only if they still haven't answered.

WhatsApp's own Remind me works differently. It fires on a timer whether or not the other person replied. Below is what each option does, the free tricks that work without any extension, and why none of this should involve bulk sending.

WhatsApp's own Remind me

In September 2025 WhatsApp added message reminders to its iPhone app, in version 25.25.74 according to MacRumors. The steps on iPhone:

  1. Long-press a message in any chat.
  2. Tap More, then Remind me.
  3. Pick 2 hours, 8 hours, 24 hours, or Custom for a date and time.

A bell icon appears on the message, and you get a notification when the time comes. 9to5Mac reported that it works in one-to-one and group chats and that Meta says reminders are private, so nobody in the chat knows you set one.

Two things to know before relying on it:

  • It's a timer on a message. It reminds you at the time you picked, even if the other person has already answered.
  • WhatsApp Web support is unclear. On November 16, 2026 we searched WhatsApp's Help Center and found no page that says whether Remind me is available on WhatsApp Web. Open the menu on a message in your own WhatsApp Web to check.

Free ways to remember a chat on WhatsApp Web

Without any extension, these built-in options help you come back to a chat:

  • Mark the chat as unread. Hover over the chat, click the down arrow and choose Mark as unread (WhatsApp's steps). It suits a message you read but can't answer yet. Our guide on marking a WhatsApp chat unread covers the shortcut and what the sender sees.
  • Pin the chat. WhatsApp's Help Center says you can pin up to three chats to the top of the list. See how to keep more than 3 chats at the top.

Both mark chats where you owe the reply. The harder case is the reverse: you sent a quote or a question, the last message in the chat is yours, and nothing in WhatsApp Web flags that the other person went quiet.

Follow-up reminders with WA Folders

WA Folders adds a side panel to WhatsApp Web in Chrome. These steps match version 2.2.2, which we checked in the extension's code on November 16, 2026.

  1. Open a one-to-one chat where you sent the last message.
  2. In the panel's This chat section, under Remind me if they don't reply, click In 1 day or In 3 days.
  3. If they reply before then, the reminder is removed. WA Folders counts a reply when an unread badge appears on their row in the chat list, or when their message is the last one in the open chat.
  4. If they don't, the chat moves to the Due list, the WA Folders toolbar icon shows a red count, and, if you allowed desktop notifications in WA Folders settings, you get a notice that says there's no reply yet.

Follow-ups work in one-to-one chats only. For a group, use a plain reminder: 1 h, 3 h, Tomorrow 9:00, Monday 9:00, or a date and time you pick. A plain reminder comes due at that time whether or not anyone wrote.

Waiting on them, without setting anything

You don't have to set a reminder on every chat. The Waiting on them list shows one-to-one chats where your message is the last one and older than the wait time you choose in Settings. It works from chats WA Folders has seen on your computer, so open a chat once with the extension running before it can appear there.

The Follow-ups list puts waiting chats and due reminders in one place. You can snooze any item for 1 day, 3 days or until Monday. Our guide to seeing who hasn't replied on WhatsApp Web walks through those lists.

Why there's no bulk sending

Some WhatsApp Web extensions schedule or send messages for you. WA Folders doesn't. A reminder puts the chat in front of you; you write the message and press Enter yourself.

That limit is on purpose. WhatsApp's Terms of Service say you must not use the service for "bulk messaging, auto-messaging, auto-dialing, and the like". A tool that only reminds you stays clear of that, and your account behaves the same as when you type every message by hand.

FAQ

Can WhatsApp remind me if someone doesn't reply?

WhatsApp's own Remind me fires at the time you pick, whether or not they answered. WA Folders on WhatsApp Web has a follow-up reminder that clears itself if they reply first, and shows the chat under Due if they don't.

Does WhatsApp Web have Remind me?

WhatsApp added Remind me to its iPhone app in September 2025. We found no WhatsApp Help Center page that says whether it's on WhatsApp Web, so check the menu on a message in your own WhatsApp Web.

Does WA Folders send follow-up messages for me?

No. It never types or sends a message. When a follow-up comes due, you open the chat, write your message and press Enter yourself.

Do WA Folders reminders work in group chats?

Plain reminders do: 1 hour, 3 hours, tomorrow at 9:00, Monday at 9:00 or a time you pick. The "if they don't reply" follow-up is for one-to-one chats only.
