WhatsApp Web keeps logging you out? The 5 usual causes and fixes

Published November 5, 2026

If WhatsApp Web keeps sending you back to the QR code, one of five things is usually behind it: your phone hasn't opened WhatsApp in over 14 days, the computer sat unused for 30 days, Stay logged in on this browser was unticked, your browser deletes site data when it closes, or someone logged the computer out from the phone. Each one has a quick check.

1. Your phone hasn't opened WhatsApp in 14 days

WhatsApp Web is a linked device. It works while your phone is off or offline, but it still depends on the phone's account. WhatsApp's Help Center says linked devices "will log out if your phone is unused for over 14 days", and the phone-number linking page puts it as needing to "log in to WhatsApp on your primary phone every 14 days to keep linked devices connected."

This catches people who use WhatsApp almost only on the computer, or who keep a second phone in a drawer for a work number.

Fix: open WhatsApp on the phone at least once every two weeks.

2. The computer sat unused for 30 days

The other timer runs on the linked device itself. WhatsApp says it will "automatically disconnect linked devices after 30 days of inactivity." An office PC you only use now and then, or a laptop that stays closed over a long break, can drop off this way.

Fix: none, other than using that browser more often. Relink when you're back.

3. "Stay logged in on this browser" was unticked

Below the QR code, WhatsApp Web shows a checkbox called Stay logged in on this browser. We loaded web.whatsapp.com in a fresh Chrome window on November 5, 2026 to check the wording, and that's the current label. Leave it ticked on your own computer and WhatsApp Web keeps your session after you close the tab. Untick it on a shared or public computer.

Fix: log out, then scan the QR code again with the box ticked.

4. Your browser deletes site data when it closes

WhatsApp Web keeps your login in the browser's site data for web.whatsapp.com. Clear that data and the browser forgets the session, so you'll see the QR code again. The usual culprits:

  • Chrome's close-all-windows setting. Under Settings, Privacy and security, Site settings, Additional content settings, On-device site data, one option reads "Delete data sites have saved to your device when you close all windows." With that on, WhatsApp Web logs out every time you close Chrome. Keep the setting and add web.whatsapp.com to the sites allowed to save data, or switch the default back.
  • Incognito or private windows. These throw away site data when you close them, so every visit starts at the QR code.
  • Clearing cookies by hand. WhatsApp's own connection-trouble checklist suggests clearing your browser cache and cookies. It works for connection problems, and it also logs you out, so expect to scan the code again afterwards.
  • Cleanup tools and privacy extensions that wipe cookies on a schedule.

5. Someone logged it out from the phone

Every linked computer shows up on the phone. On Android, tap More (three dots), then Linked devices. On iPhone, go to Settings, then Linked devices. Tapping a device and choosing Log out ends that session at once.

Look at this list whenever you get logged out for no clear reason. A device you don't recognise means someone else had access to your account. Log it out, and read our guide to WhatsApp Web malware and linked devices.

WhatsApp allows up to four linked devices at a time, so the list is also where you clear out old computers you no longer use. If you forgot to log out of a hotel or shared PC, the same screen fixes it: see left WhatsApp Web logged in on a hotel PC.

If you're in India

India's SIM binding rule once included a six-hour WhatsApp Web logout. The government dropped the fixed timer in April 2026 and moved to risk-based logouts. Our SIM binding guide has the details and dates.

Will I lose my chats when WhatsApp Web logs out?

No. Your messages live on your phone, and WhatsApp Web syncs them again when you relink. Older messages can take a while to show up on the computer. If some days stay missing, see WhatsApp Web not showing older messages.

Being logged out of the WhatsApp app on your phone is a different problem. If that happened, start with logged out of WhatsApp and your chats are gone.

FAQ

Why does WhatsApp Web log me out every day?

Most often the browser is deleting site data when it closes, or you're in an incognito window. Check Chrome's On-device site data setting and tick Stay logged in on this browser the next time you scan.

Does WhatsApp Web work if my phone is off?

Yes. WhatsApp says linked devices work without your phone online. The phone has to open WhatsApp at least once every 14 days or the computer gets logged out.

How many computers can use WhatsApp Web at once?

Up to four linked devices at a time, counting computers, tablets and other linked devices.

Can I stay logged in on a work computer?

Yes, if you tick Stay logged in on this browser and the company's browser policy doesn't wipe site data. If IT clears browser data every night, you'll have to scan the code each morning.
