WhatsApp Events can now invite people who aren't on WhatsApp

Published November 7, 2026

WhatsApp Events can now reach people who do not have the app. You create the event, add a cover photo, share a link, and guests can open that link to see the details and RSVP without installing WhatsApp. Meta also added an Events Home under Settings so you can create invites, manage RSVPs, set custom reminders, and allow a plus-one from one place.

The Meta Newsroom post (dated 5 and 6 November 2026) and the matching WhatsApp Blog describe the same update. The Verge covered it the same day. The feature is rolling out over the coming weeks, so you may not see every option on your account yet.

What changed

Before this update, a WhatsApp event lived inside one chat. Guests had to be in that individual or group chat to see it and reply. WhatsApp’s create-events help page still documents that chat-bound flow, including the older note that you cannot invite people who are not in the chat and cannot forward an event to another chat. The Newsroom update adds a shareable link on top of Events, so people outside WhatsApp can open the invite in a browser.

In short:

  • Shareable invite link for people who do not have WhatsApp. They can view details and RSVP without downloading the app.
  • Events Home at Settings → Events, listing past and upcoming events you created or were invited to.
  • Custom reminders and a plus-one option when you send invites.
  • Cover photo on the invite.

How to create and share an event invite

Exact button labels can vary while the update rolls out. Meta’s announcement describes this path:

  1. Create an event in WhatsApp (from a chat’s attachment menu → Event, or from Settings → Events once Events Home is on your account).
  2. Add a name, date and time. Optionally add a description, end time, location, cover photo, custom reminder, and whether guests can bring a plus-one.
  3. Share the invite link with people who are not on WhatsApp (text, email, another app, or whatever channel they actually check).

For the older chat-based create flow that still works inside a group or one-to-one chat, WhatsApp’s create and edit events page lists the fields: name, date and time, description (up to 2,048 characters), end time, location, optional WhatsApp call link, and “Allow guests” for a plus-one. Only one person hosts an event; there is no co-host. If disappearing messages are on, you may not get the full calendar until you turn that off.

Once the event is in a chat, people already in that chat can open the event message and respond Going, Maybe, or Not going. WhatsApp’s respond to events help page also lists Going with guest when that option is available. People who answer Going, Going with guest, or Maybe get a notification if you edit or cancel the event.

What guests without WhatsApp can do

According to Meta:

  • They open the shared link.
  • They can see the event details and RSVP without installing WhatsApp or creating an account.
  • They only see the event address after they RSVP.
  • Your phone number is not shown to guests you do not know.

Personal messages and calls stay end-to-end encrypted. The invite page is for event details and RSVP, not a replacement for private chat.

Where to find Events Home

Open WhatsApp → Settings → Events. Meta says that screen shows past and upcoming events you created or were invited to, and is where you create customized invites and manage RSVPs. If Events is missing under Settings, the rollout has not reached your account yet. Wait for an app update and check again over the next few weeks.

You can also open events from a chat: tap or click the chat name, then Events, as described on the respond-to-events help page. That only shows events for chats you belong to.

Privacy notes

From Meta’s announcement:

  • Phone number is hidden from guests you do not know.
  • Non-WhatsApp guests see the address only after they RSVP.
  • Personal messages and calls remain end-to-end encrypted.

Treat the invite link like any other RSVP page: share it with the people you mean to invite, not in a public post if the location is private.

Late joiners still cannot see an old group event

WhatsApp’s own help pages state this clearly: new members cannot see or join events created before they joined the group, even if the event is pinned. The creator has to delete the old event and create a new one after those people join if they need the RSVP card inside the group.

That limit is separate from the new outside-WhatsApp link. The shareable link helps you reach cousins, clients or friends who never install WhatsApp. It does not, by itself, fix visibility for someone who joins your family group a week after you posted the event inside the chat. For late group joiners, recreate the event (or send them the shareable link once that option is on your account).

People on r/whatsapp have reported the same late-joiner blind spot for years; WhatsApp’s FAQ now matches those reports.

Rollout caveat

Meta says the updated Events experience is rolling out to everyone over the following weeks. If you do not see a shareable link, Events Home, cover photo, or plus-one controls yet:

  1. Update WhatsApp on your phone.
  2. Check Settings → Events.
  3. Try creating an event from a chat’s attachment menu and look for a share or link option.
  4. If nothing new appears, wait a few days and try again. The Newsroom post does not give a hard end date.

Quick FAQ

Can someone RSVP without installing WhatsApp? Yes, once the shareable-link update has reached the host’s account. Meta says guests can view details and RSVP without downloading the app.

Where is Events Home? Settings → Events, per Meta and The Verge.

Will guests without WhatsApp see my phone number? Meta says your phone number is not shown to guests you do not know.

When do they see the address? Only after they RSVP, for guests without a WhatsApp account.

Someone joined my group after I posted the event. Why can’t they see it? WhatsApp’s help pages say new members cannot see events created before they joined. Delete and recreate the event, or share the invite link with them once that option is available.

Is this on every account today? No. Meta says it is rolling out over the coming weeks.
