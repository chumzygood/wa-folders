WhatsApp is swapping the Communities tab for Contacts: where it went

Published November 11, 2026

If the Communities tab vanished from the bottom of your WhatsApp and a Contacts tab showed up in its place, your communities are still there: WhatsApp moved them to a filter at the top of the Chats tab. This is a beta change that is rolling out to some testers on Android and iPhone, so not everyone sees it yet.

What changed

WABetaInfo reported on November 9 that WhatsApp beta for Android 2.26.45.2 replaces the Communities tab in the bottom bar with a new Contacts tab. On November 10 it reported the same change in WhatsApp beta for iOS 26.44.10.70 on TestFlight. In both cases WABetaInfo says the feature is available to some beta testers and will reach more accounts over the coming weeks. WhatsApp has not said when it reaches everyone.

WABetaInfo's explanation is that WhatsApp already added a filter in the chat list for communities, which made a separate tab unnecessary.

Where your communities went

Open the Chats tab and look at the filter chips above your chat list. Per WABetaInfo, your communities are reached through a filter there instead of their own tab. Nothing was deleted. Your community groups and announcement groups are the same chats they were before; only the shortcut moved.

If you don't see the new layout, you don't have the test yet, and the Communities tab works as before.

What the new Contacts tab shows

According to WABetaInfo, the Contacts tab has:

  • a Favorites section at the top for the contacts you marked as favorites
  • your full contact list, sortable by alphabetical, activity or birthdays
  • on activity, people who are online right now at the top with a green dot, and everyone else sorted by last seen
  • a button to add a new contact, with fields for first name, last name, phone number and username

WABetaInfo says the online and last seen details only appear when a contact shares them, and you won't see anyone's if you hide your own. If you'd rather not show up at the top of other people's activity sort, our guide to hiding your online status covers both settings you need to change.

WABetaInfo also says WhatsApp is testing other layouts, including one where the Contacts tab could replace the Updates tab, and that some accounts already have a Meta AI tab where Communities used to be. Your bottom bar may look different from a friend's for a while.

Keeping chats sorted on your phone

With communities folded into the Chats tab, the filter row is doing more of the sorting work. WhatsApp's own tool for this is Lists. The Help Center calls them customizable filters: swipe down on the Chats tab to see them. To make one, go to Settings > Lists > New list, name it and add people or groups. A list needs at least one chat in it.

A Work list and a Family list next to the communities filter gets you most of the way to folders on your phone. Our guide to keeping work and personal chats apart walks through it.

What this means on WhatsApp Web

WABetaInfo's two reports cover the Android and iPhone beta apps and don't mention WhatsApp Web. The one thing that already differs on the web is Lists: WhatsApp's Lists help page for the web app says the feature isn't available there.

FAQ

Did WhatsApp remove Communities?

No. In the beta WABetaInfo describes, WhatsApp removed the Communities tab from the bottom bar and kept communities in a filter in the Chats tab. Your communities and their groups are still there.

Why don't I have the Contacts tab?

It's rolling out gradually to some beta testers on Android 2.26.45.2 and iOS 26.44.10.70. WABetaInfo says more accounts get it over the coming weeks, and WhatsApp hasn't announced a date for everyone.

Can people see when I'm online in the Contacts tab?

Only if you share your online status and last seen. WABetaInfo says the tab shows activity only for contacts who allow it, and hides other people's if you hide yours.

Can I get the Communities tab back?

WhatsApp hasn't offered a setting for that in the reports so far. Use the communities filter at the top of the Chats tab instead.

Does WhatsApp Web have Lists?

Not according to WhatsApp's Help Center, which says Lists aren't available on the web app. A Chrome extension such as WA Folders can add folder tabs to WhatsApp Web instead.
