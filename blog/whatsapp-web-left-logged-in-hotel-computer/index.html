Left WhatsApp Web logged in on a hotel PC? Log it out from your phone

Published November 7, 2026

If you left WhatsApp Web signed in on a hotel, library or office computer, open WhatsApp on your phone, go to Linked devices, tap that computer and choose Log out; you don't need to go back to the machine. Closing the tab or the browser doesn't end the session on its own, so do this even if you're sure you shut the window.

Why closing the tab didn't log you out

WhatsApp Web is a linked device. Once your phone scans the QR code, that browser connects to your account by itself, and WhatsApp's Help Center says linked devices work without your phone online.

Below the QR code there's a box called Stay logged in on this browser. WhatsApp's security page says you can untick it before scanning "so your WhatsApp session is automatically logged out if you close the tab or browser." Most people never touch it, so on a shared PC the session usually survives the closed window. Whoever opens WhatsApp Web in that same browser profile afterwards can land in your chats.

How to log out a hotel or shared PC from your phone

WhatsApp's unlink steps are the same whether the computer is across the room or in another country:

  1. Open WhatsApp on your primary phone.
  2. On Android, tap the three-dot menu, then Linked devices. On iPhone, open Settings (or tap your profile picture), then Linked devices.
  3. Tap the computer you want to remove.
  4. Tap Log out.

If you think someone got into your account through WhatsApp Web, WhatsApp says you can log out of all your active WhatsApp Web sessions from your phone. Do that, then link your own computer again.

How long a forgotten session stays connected

Two WhatsApp rules cut a forgotten session off, and neither is quick:

  • 30 days of no use on that computer. The Help Center says WhatsApp will "automatically disconnect linked devices after 30 days of inactivity." If someone keeps using that browser, it isn't inactive.
  • 14 days without your phone. Linked devices "will log out if your phone is unused for over 14 days." That only happens if you stop opening WhatsApp on your phone, which most people don't.

So a session on a busy hotel lobby PC can outlast your stay by weeks. WhatsApp also notes it "can't provide information about who accessed your account or the time and location it was accessed," so the Linked devices list is the only check you get. Log it out yourself instead of waiting for a timer.

Audit your linked devices after any trip

WhatsApp lets you link up to four devices to one phone, and old ones pile up. Its Help Center advice for keeping your account safer comes down to three habits:

  • Check Linked devices regularly and log out anything you don't recognise or no longer use.
  • Keep push notifications on. When a new device links, the notification lets you review and remove it. WhatsApp also asks you to confirm when it detects an unrecognised device trying to link.
  • Turn on two-step verification for an extra PIN on your account.

On a shared computer next time, untick Stay logged in on this browser before you scan, and log out from the WhatsApp Web menu before you leave. For a computer you use every day, our guide to locking WhatsApp Web with a password covers app lock and chat lock.

If an unknown device keeps coming back after you log it out, treat it as a compromise rather than a forgotten session and read WhatsApp Web malware and linked devices.

FAQ

Does closing the browser log me out of WhatsApp Web?

Only if you unticked Stay logged in on this browser before scanning the QR code. Otherwise the session stays linked after you close the tab or the browser, until you log it out.

How do I log out of WhatsApp Web remotely?

On your phone, open WhatsApp, go to Linked devices, tap the computer and tap Log out. On Android, Linked devices is under the three-dot menu; on iPhone, it's under Settings.

Will WhatsApp log out a forgotten computer by itself?

Eventually. WhatsApp disconnects linked devices after 30 days of inactivity, and logs them out if your phone goes unused for over 14 days. A computer someone keeps using isn't inactive, so log it out yourself.

Can I see who read my chats on the hotel computer?

No. WhatsApp says it can't provide information about who accessed your account or when and where it was accessed. You can see that the device is linked, and log it out.

Does logging out the hotel PC delete my chats?

No. Logging out ends that computer's access. Your chats stay on your phone, and you can link the computer again later.
