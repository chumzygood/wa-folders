How to save all the photos, voice notes, videos and documents from a WhatsApp Web chat

Published November 27, 2026 · Em português

WhatsApp gives you two built-in ways to keep photos, voice notes, videos and documents from a chat. You can open the chat's info, go to Media, links and docs, and download items one at a time; or, on your phone, export the chat with media. If you already use WhatsApp Web, Files in WA Folders 2.4.1 (Pro) is a faster way to search, zip and save copies after WhatsApp Web has loaded the file.

Download one item at a time from Media, links and docs

On WhatsApp Web, open the chat and click the contact or group name at the top to open the chat info. Open Media, links and docs (WhatsApp's Help Center uses this name on its How to delete media page, checked November 27, 2026). Pick a photo, voice note, video or document, then save or download it. You do this once per file.

The same idea applies in the WhatsApp Desktop app for Mac and Windows. WhatsApp's Help Center article How to download media files on Mac and Windows computers says you click the media file, open the menu and choose Save to downloads, and that "until an image is manually downloaded, it won't be saved permanently on your computer."

That works well when you only need a few files. It is slow if you want a whole chat's media, because there is no download-all control in that gallery.

If you are trying to find one photo, link or document in a long history, see how to find a photo, link or document in WhatsApp. If you want WhatsApp Desktop to stop saving files on its own, see how to turn off auto-download on WhatsApp Desktop.

Export the chat with media from your phone

WhatsApp's own export can pack the chat text and, on a phone, some or all of the media into a file you keep on your computer. The steps differ by device (WhatsApp Help Center, How to export your chat history, checked November 27, 2026):

  • Android. Open the chat, tap More options > More > Export chat, then choose Include media or Without media, and pick an app to send it with, such as Gmail. If you choose Include media, WhatsApp says "the most recent media sent will be added as attachments." It does not say every file in the chat is included.
  • iPhone. Tap the contact or group name > Export chat. If the chat has media, choose With all media, With selected media, or Without media. The export is a ZIP file. View once messages are not exported.
  • WhatsApp Web, Windows and Mac. Open the chat menu > Export chat, choose All messages or a custom date range, then Export. It is saved as a ZIP file. Media isn't included.

The file is a text log, not a backup, and WhatsApp says it can't be re-imported. To read that .txt or ZIP, see WhatsApp exported chat viewer: how to read the .txt file.

FAQ

Does WhatsApp Web have a button to download every photo in a chat at once?

No. In the chat info, Media, links and docs lets you open and download items one at a time. WhatsApp's export on Web, Windows and Mac does not include media. On a phone, you can export the chat with media; on Android, WhatsApp says the most recent media is added as attachments, not necessarily every file.

Does WA Folders Files download everything automatically?

No. Files keeps a copy only after WhatsApp Web has loaded the file (you open the photo or play the voice note), or after you import a phone chat ZIP with media. It does not download a chat's media in the background.

What does Listing only mean in Files?

The file is listed, but WA Folders has not kept a copy yet. Open or play the media in WhatsApp Web, import a phone chat ZIP with media, or choose the original file from your computer.

Can Files email a file for me?

Email as draft (Pro) opens a new Gmail, Outlook or Yahoo Mail draft for a file you select. In Gmail and Outlook, WA Folders attaches it after you click Attach (up to 20 MiB). In Yahoo Mail you attach the downloaded file. Nothing is sent; you review the draft and press Send yourself.

Is Files included in the free plan?

No. Files, Rename and save, and Email as draft are Pro, which is US$4.99 once, with no subscription and no trial. The free plan still includes 10 folders, the first 50 results for each search and 10 exports a day, with no time limit.
