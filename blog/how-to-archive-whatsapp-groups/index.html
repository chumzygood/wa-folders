How to archive WhatsApp groups (there's no auto-archive yet)

Published November 21, 2026

To archive a WhatsApp group, hover over it on WhatsApp Web, click the arrow and choose Archive chat (on Android, tap and hold it and tap the archive icon; on iPhone, swipe left and tap Archive), because WhatsApp has no setting that archives inactive groups for you.

That last part is what most people go looking for. On November 20, 2026 a WhatsApp user on X suggested that groups should archive themselves after a period of inactivity, since most groups "become irrelevant after a certain point." As of November 21, 2026, WhatsApp's archive help page describes only manual archiving, on every platform. So clearing out dead groups is a one-time job you do by hand. Here's how to do it without losing anything you might want later.

Archive a group on WhatsApp Web

  1. Hover over the group in your chat list.
  2. Click the small arrow that appears on the right.
  3. Click Archive chat.

The group moves into Archived, which sits at the top of your chats. WhatsApp's help page for Web only shows archiving one chat at a time.

Archive groups on your phone

  • Android: in the Chats tab, tap and hold the group, then tap the archive icon at the top.
  • iPhone: in the Chats tab, swipe left on the group and tap Archive.

Both phone apps also have an Archive all chats option. On Android it's under Settings > Chats > Chat history; on iPhone, under Settings > Chats. It archives every chat, including the people and groups you talk to every day, so it only helps if you plan to unarchive the few you need. Most people with 50 dead groups and 10 live ones are better off archiving the dead ones one by one.

What archiving a group does, and what it doesn't

WhatsApp's help pages say:

  • Archiving doesn't delete the group or back it up. You're still a member, and new messages keep arriving.
  • An archived chat stays archived when a new message comes in, unless Keep chats archived is turned off on your phone. If your archived groups keep popping back up, that setting is off; here's where to turn it on.
  • You won't get notifications for archived chats unless someone mentions you or replies to you. You can't change notification settings for archived chats.
  • The number next to Archived counts archived chats with unread messages, and an @ appears there when someone mentions or replies to you in an archived group.

That means archiving is the right move for a group you might need again: the school year group from last year, a trip that's over, a project that ended. To get one back, see how to find and unarchive archived chats.

Archive, mute or exit: which one for each group

Going through a long list is faster with one rule per group.

The group is... Do this What happens
Finished, but you might want the history Archive Out of your list, no alerts unless you're mentioned
Still useful, just noisy Mute for Always Stays in your list, phone stops buzzing
Something you'll never read again Exit group You leave and keep the old messages; admins see that you left
Gone for good, history included Exit and delete for me You leave, and the group and its history are removed from your chats

Muting gives you three lengths: 8 hours, 1 week or Always. WhatsApp's mute page notes that muting a group on your phone also mutes it on WhatsApp Web and Desktop.

Before you exit, know what the other members see. WhatsApp's exit page says group admins see a "left" message, and you can't turn that off. Your name and number also show in the group's Past members list for up to 60 days. If the group belongs to a community, exiting it also takes you out of the community and its other groups. A deleted group can't be restored; to get back in, you'd need an admin to invite you again. Our guide to leaving a WhatsApp group quietly goes through this in more detail.

If you were in a scam or fraud group, WhatsApp suggests choosing Exit group rather than Exit and delete for me, so the messages stay as evidence.

Stop new groups from piling up

Exiting doesn't stop people from adding you to new groups. WhatsApp's group privacy page says that by default anyone with your number can add you. Change Groups to My contacts or My contacts except... in your phone's privacy settings. A group admin outside that list can then only send you a private invite, and you have three days to accept it before it expires. You can only change this on your phone; WhatsApp Web and Desktop pick up the setting. Step-by-step: how to stop people adding you to WhatsApp groups.

FAQ

Can WhatsApp archive inactive groups automatically?

No. As of November 21, 2026, WhatsApp's help pages for Web, Android and iPhone describe only manual archiving. You archive each group yourself, or use Archive all chats on your phone and unarchive the ones you need.

Does archiving a WhatsApp group mean I left it?

No. You stay a member and still receive its messages. The group moves to Archived, and you only get notified if someone mentions you or replies to you.

Will the group know I archived it?

WhatsApp's help pages don't describe any notice to members when you archive a chat. Exiting is different: admins see a "left" message and your details show under Past members for up to 60 days.

Why do my archived groups keep coming back?

Keep chats archived is turned off on your phone. With it off, a new message moves the chat back out of Archived. Turn it on in Settings > Chats on Android or iPhone.

Can I archive several WhatsApp groups at once?

On your phone, Archive all chats archives everything at once, and on iPhone you can select several archived chats to unarchive together. WhatsApp's Web help page shows archiving one chat at a time.
