How to make your own WhatsApp sticker on WhatsApp Web (from a photo)

Published November 14, 2026

You don't need a sticker app to turn a photo into a WhatsApp sticker. WhatsApp Web has had a sticker maker built in since November 2021, when The Verge reported it arriving on the web first and on the Desktop apps a week later. On a computer it's often the easier place to do it, because you trace the cutout with a mouse instead of a finger, and the photo is often already on your computer.

Make a sticker on WhatsApp Web

  1. Open web.whatsapp.com and open any chat. If you only want to make the sticker and keep it for later, open your chat with yourself so nobody else gets your test versions.
  2. Click the attach button to the left of the message box. Depending on your version it's a + or a paperclip.
  3. Choose New sticker (older versions call it Sticker). You can also open the emoji panel, switch to the stickers tab and click Create.
  4. Pick an image from your computer. When Popular Science tested the tool, it accepted JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF files, but GIFs came out as still images.
  5. Edit it in the editor that opens (details below).
  6. Click the green Send arrow. The sticker goes into the chat you have open.

The editing tools

The editor has these tools:

  • Outline (scissors icon). Trace around the person, pet or object you want to keep, and WhatsApp drops the rest of the background. iDownloadBlog notes the cutout also gets a white border, the classic sticker look.
  • Crop. Crops to a square, per The Verge. Use it when you want the whole photo instead of a cutout.
  • Emoji and stickers. Drop an emoji or another sticker on top.
  • Text (T icon) and pen for captions and doodles.
  • Undo and redo arrows.

To move a layer, drag it. Popular Science describes two handles on a selected item: the top one rotates it and the corner one resizes it, and guide lines appear to help you center things.

If the subject is small, zoom the browser in (Ctrl and +) before you trace it.

Where your new sticker goes

You don't save the sticker anywhere. Sending it once is enough. WhatsApp puts it in your recent stickers, and both Popular Science and iDownloadBlog say it shows up on your phone too, under the same account.

To keep a sticker easy to find, add it to favorites. On a computer, click the sticker in the chat, or hover over it and click the small arrow in its corner, then choose Add to Favorites. On the phone, tap the sticker in the chat, or press and hold it in the sticker tray.

To get rid of a test version, press and hold it in the tray and pick Remove from Recent. iDownloadBlog points out that a sticker you've also favorited stays in Favorites until you remove it there as well.

Can you make an animated sticker from a GIF?

Not with WhatsApp Web's sticker maker. An animated GIF goes in as a single still frame.

Animated stickers do exist, but they normally come in sticker packs made outside WhatsApp. WhatsApp's Help Center lists the rules for those packs: each animated sticker is 512 x 512 pixels, 500 KB at most, and no longer than 10 seconds, and a pack can't mix animated and still stickers. If you want to send a GIF as a moving image, the GIF tab in the emoji panel is the simpler route.

Making stickers on your phone instead

  • iPhone: open a chat, tap the sticker icon, then the create option and Use a photo, per WhatsApp's iPhone help page. WhatsApp suggests a cutout, and you can add text, emoji or drawings. The Verge reported that creating stickers needs iOS 17 or later. On older versions you can edit stickers but not make new ones.
  • Android: tap the sticker icon, then Create and pick a photo from your gallery or take a new one, per the Android help page.

Both help pages also describe Generate with AI, which makes up to four stickers from a short description. WhatsApp says it's only available in some countries. Descriptions work in English, Indonesian, Portuguese and Spanish on Android, and only English on iPhone. The web version of the same help article says that feature isn't available on WhatsApp Web.

Text stickers are getting easier to find

If you want a sticker made of words rather than a photo, WhatsApp on Android already turns text you type into the sticker search into styled stickers. On November 13, WABetaInfo reported that WhatsApp beta for Android 2.26.46.1 adds a text sticker option right next to the photo option in the sticker keyboard, and raises the number of designs from five to ten, including a post-it note and a handwritten style. It's rolling out to some beta testers first. WhatsApp hasn't said when everyone gets it, and the report doesn't mention WhatsApp Web.

FAQ

Why don't I see New sticker on WhatsApp Web?

Reload the page first so you're on the latest version of WhatsApp Web. If the attach menu still doesn't show it, try the emoji panel, open the stickers tab and look for Create.

Can the other person tell I made the sticker myself?

They see it like any other sticker. They can tap it to add it to their own favorites.

Do stickers I make on the computer work on my phone?

Yes. Once you've sent it, the sticker shows up in your recent stickers on your phone as well. Favorite it if you want it to stay there.
