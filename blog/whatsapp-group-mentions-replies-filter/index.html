WhatsApp Mentions and replies filter: find where you were tagged

Published October 24, 2026

WhatsApp's new Mentions and replies filter shows every unread group chat where someone tagged you, replied to one of your messages or posted an @all, so you can open those first. WhatsApp announced it on October 29, 2026, and it's reaching accounts gradually. Because it only lists unread chats, you still need the search tricks further down to find a mention you've already scrolled past.

What the Mentions and replies filter does

In its October 29 blog post, WhatsApp describes "a new chat filter that shows every unread group conversation where you were either mentioned or received a direct reply, including @all messages." It sits at the top of the Chats tab with the other filters such as Unread, Favorites and Groups.

WABetaInfo first spotted it in WhatsApp beta for Android 2.26.42.12 on October 24 and added a few details:

  • It covers community groups as well as regular groups.
  • It carries a badge with the number of chats that still have pending mentions or replies.
  • When nothing is pending, it shows an empty screen with a link back to the full list.
  • It's optional. You can move it into the "More" section of the filter bar if you don't want it in the main row.

The filter came out in the same announcement as forever pins, sending message history to people after they join, and group status. If an important message in the group was pinned rather than tagged, see how to pin a WhatsApp message forever.

Who has it

WABetaInfo says the update is rolling out on the latest WhatsApp for Android and iOS, to some users first and more accounts "over the coming weeks." Neither WhatsApp's post nor WABetaInfo mentions WhatsApp Web or the desktop apps, so don't expect it there yet. If you don't see it on your phone, update WhatsApp from the Play Store or App Store and check again later.

Find a mention without the filter

Until the filter reaches you, or once you've already read the chat, these methods work in the regular app and on WhatsApp Web.

1. Check the @ before you open the chat

In your chats list, an unread group with an @ next to it has a mention or a reply waiting for you. The WhatsApp Help Center says the marker covers both. If you're scanning a long list, the Unread filter at the top of the Chats tab cuts it down to the chats where the @ can appear.

2. Search the group for your number

Once the @ is gone, search inside the group. On Android, open the group, tap the three-dot menu and choose Search. On iPhone, tap the group name and then Search. On WhatsApp Web, open the group and click the magnifying glass at the top of the chat.

A tip that comes up on r/whatsapp is to type @ followed by your full number with the country code and no spaces or plus sign, for example @15551234567 for a US number. Results aren't consistent across phones and versions, so if nothing turns up, try the next method before you decide there's no mention.

3. Search for your name, in every form people use

A popular r/LifeProTips post suggests searching a busy group for your own name instead of scrolling. Search the name people actually call you, and also a nickname or a short form. When someone types @ and picks you from the list, other members see you under whatever name they saved you as, so the text in a message can vary.

4. Find replies to your messages

Replies are harder to search, because the reply's text doesn't contain your name. Open the group and scroll to your own last message. Replies to it show a small quoted copy of your message above the new text, and tapping that quote jumps back to the original. If you're often asked follow-up questions, star the reply when you see it (long-press, then the star) so it's in Starred messages later.

When the message you need didn't mention you at all, see how to find important messages buried in a WhatsApp group.

Muted groups still reach you through mentions

Many people mute big groups and assume they'll miss everything. According to the WhatsApp Help Center, a mention overrides the mute you set for the group, unless you've also muted @all mentions. So a direct @ to you still notifies you in a muted group, while an @all announcement only does if you haven't silenced those. If you want fewer pings from a group without missing tags meant for you, mute the group and turn off @all mentions, and keep personal mentions on.

If you run a large group

In big communities a mention can scroll out of sight in minutes, which is the case WABetaInfo says the filter is built for. If you're an admin who gets tagged with questions, reply in a thread with WhatsApp's reply feature rather than a new message, so the person who asked gets the @ marker and the chat shows up in their Mentions and replies filter while it's unread.

FAQ

What does the WhatsApp Mentions and replies filter show?

It lists every unread group chat where someone mentioned you, replied to one of your messages, or sent an @all message. WhatsApp announced it on October 29, 2026.

Why don't I have the Mentions and replies filter yet?

WhatsApp is rolling it out gradually on Android and iPhone, and WABetaInfo says more accounts get it over the coming weeks. Keep WhatsApp updated and check the filter row at the top of the Chats tab.

Is the filter on WhatsApp Web?

WhatsApp's announcement doesn't mention WhatsApp Web or desktop. On WhatsApp Web, look for the @ next to an unread group in your chats list, or search inside the group.

Does the filter show chats I've already read?

WhatsApp says it shows unread group conversations. Once you've read the chat, search the group for your name or number, or scroll to your last message to find replies.

Will I get a mention in a muted group?

Yes. The WhatsApp Help Center says a mention overrides the mute you set for the group, unless you've also muted @all mentions.
