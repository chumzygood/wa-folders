How to message yourself on WhatsApp Web (and keep that chat at the top)

Published October 31, 2026

A chat with yourself is the quickest place to drop a link, a phone number or a photo you want on your other devices. WhatsApp calls it "Message yourself", and it works on WhatsApp Web the same way it does on your phone.

The Help Center says these messages "work and look like regular chats" and are end-to-end encrypted.

Message yourself on WhatsApp Web

  1. In the Chats tab, click New chat (the speech bubble with a plus sign).
  2. Search for your own name or phone number, or pick it from the list.
  3. Click your name or number. The chat opens and you can type, paste links or attach files like in any other chat.

Forward a message to yourself on WhatsApp Web

  1. Open the chat that has the message.
  2. Hover over the message, click the down arrow, then Forward.
  3. Tick the box next to that message (and any others you want), then click the forward arrow.
  4. Search for your name or number and tick it.
  5. Click Send.

On Android

  1. In the Chats tab, tap New chat.
  2. Search for or tap your name or phone number.

To forward something in: tap and hold the message, tap the forward arrow, pick your name or number and tap Send.

On iPhone

  1. In the Chats tab, tap New chat (the plus sign in a circle).
  2. Search for or tap your name or phone number.

To forward something in: tap and hold the message, tap Forward, select any other messages, tap the forward arrow, pick yourself and tap Forward. The iPhone steps let you forward several messages at once.

Keep the chat at the top

The Android and iPhone help pages suggest pinning the self-chat so it's easy to find. On WhatsApp Web:

  1. Click the chat, then click the down arrow.
  2. Click Pin.
  3. Choose how long the pin should last, then click Pin.

WhatsApp allows up to three pinned chats. If you already use all three, see how to keep more than 3 chats at the top.

What the self-chat can't do

WhatsApp lists these limits for messages to yourself:

  • No audio or video calls.
  • You can't mute its notifications.
  • You can't block or report yourself.
  • No last seen or online status.

FAQ

Can I message myself on WhatsApp Web without my phone nearby? The web steps above start the chat from WhatsApp Web itself. You need WhatsApp Web to be linked to your account already.

I can't find my name in New chat. What do I search for? WhatsApp says to search for your name or your phone number. Try the number if the name doesn't come up.

Is there a better place than the self-chat for one important message? If the message is already in another chat, you can star it instead: hover over it, click the down arrow and choose Star. Starred messages are listed under Starred messages above your chats list. More in where starred messages are on WhatsApp Web.
