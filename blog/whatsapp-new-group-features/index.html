WhatsApp's 4 new group chat features (Oct 2026) and what works on Web

Published November 2, 2026

On October 29, 2026, WhatsApp announced four group chat updates: messages you can pin forever, message history you can send after someone has joined, a group status only that group can see, and a chat filter for unread groups where you were mentioned or replied to. They are rolling out gradually on Android and iPhone, and neither WhatsApp nor WABetaInfo says anything about WhatsApp Web yet.

WhatsApp's announcement pitches all four as ways to "pick up where you left off" in a busy group. WABetaInfo lists the compatible versions as the latest WhatsApp for Android and iOS, reaching some users first and more accounts "over the coming weeks." If you don't see a feature, update the app and check again in a few days. There is nothing to switch on.

1. Pin a message forever

Pins used to expire after 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days. You can now keep a pinned message at the top of the chat with no time limit. That suits group rules, an address or a standing schedule. We walk through pinning on each device, and why some members can't see an older pin, in how to pin a WhatsApp message forever.

2. Send message history after someone joins

Since February 2026 you could toggle on message history when adding someone, so they see recent messages instead of an empty chat. Now you can also send it later by tapping the new member's name in the chat. WhatsApp says the messages stay end-to-end encrypted. Limits, admin permissions and what gets left out are covered in how to send message history to new group members.

3. Group status

A group status goes only to the members of one group. WhatsApp's example is that "what goes in the running club chat might not belong in the family chat." Everyone in the group can view it, react and add their own status, and like a regular status it disappears after 24 hours.

This is different from a status mention. The Help Center describes mentions as tagging up to 5 people or groups in your normal status, which still reaches your usual status audience. A group status stays inside the group. WhatsApp hasn't published a Help Center page with step-by-step instructions for group status yet, so expect the exact buttons to vary while it rolls out.

4. Mentions and replies filter

A new filter in the chat list shows every unread group conversation where someone mentioned you, replied to you or posted an @all. Because it only lists unread chats, a mention you've already scrolled past won't appear there. Search tips for those are in WhatsApp's Mentions and replies filter.

Which of these work on WhatsApp Web?

As of November 2, 2026, WhatsApp's post doesn't name any platform, and WABetaInfo lists only Android and iOS. Pins and message history already existed on WhatsApp Web before this update, so check there after your phone gets the new options. For group status and the new filter, assume phone first.

On WhatsApp Web today you can still catch up on a busy group the older ways: look for the @ marker next to an unread group in the chat list, and use the magnifying glass at the top of a group to search inside it. If you follow many groups, finding important messages in a WhatsApp group has more methods.

FAQ

When did WhatsApp announce these group features?

On October 29, 2026, in the WhatsApp Blog post "Never miss what matters in your group chats."

Why don't I have them yet?

WhatsApp is rolling them out gradually. WABetaInfo says some accounts get them first and more will over the coming weeks. Keep WhatsApp updated.

Who can see a WhatsApp group status?

Only members of that group. Everyone in it can view, react and add their own status, and it disappears after 24 hours.

Does the Mentions and replies filter show messages I already read?

No. WhatsApp describes it as showing unread group conversations where you were mentioned or got a direct reply, including @all messages.

Are these features on WhatsApp Web?

The announcement doesn't say, and WABetaInfo lists Android and iOS only. On Web, use the @ marker in the chat list and search inside the group in the meantime.
