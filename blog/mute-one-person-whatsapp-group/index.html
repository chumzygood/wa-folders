Can you mute one person in a WhatsApp group? What you can do instead

Published November 18, 2026 · Em português

Someone in the group shares the same kind of post twenty times a day, and you'd like to stop seeing it without leaving. WhatsApp has no switch for that. Mute works on a whole individual or group chat, with a choice of 8 hours, 1 week or Always, and you still receive every message; your device just stops making noise about it (WhatsApp Help Center).

Blocking doesn't do it either. WhatsApp says blocking a contact "won't stop that user from seeing your messages in a group you both participate in, and you'll see their messages in that group as well" (WhatsApp Help Center). Below is what you can do instead, first as a member, then as an admin.

If you're a member

Mute the group

On WhatsApp Web (WhatsApp Help Center):

  1. Open the group.
  2. Click the group name at the top.
  3. Turn on Mute notifications.
  4. Pick 8 hours, 1 week or Always, then click Mute.

If you mute a group on your phone, it's muted on WhatsApp Web and the desktop app too. The trade-off is that the group goes quiet for everyone in it, not just the person you wanted to tune out. Our post on muting a chat forever has the phone steps, and the Mentions and replies filter shows how to find the messages where someone tagged you.

Delete their messages on your side

You can remove a message you received with Delete for me: hover over it, click the arrow, then Delete > Delete for me. It only clears your copy. Everyone else still sees it, and you have 5 seconds to click Undo before it's gone for good (WhatsApp Help Center). That's fine for one forwarded video, but there's no way to apply it to everything one person sends.

Flag it to the admins

If the admins have turned on Send for admin review, click and hold the message, then Send for admin review > OK. All admins are told. The person who sent the message and the other members are not (WhatsApp Help Center). If the option isn't there, message an admin directly; the group info panel shows Admin next to their names.

Report or leave

Reporting is for spam, scams and abuse rather than a member who simply posts too much. If you report the whole group, WhatsApp removes you from it, and an admin has to invite you back if you want to return (WhatsApp Help Center). If it comes to leaving, leaving a group quietly covers what the others see.

If you're an admin

  • Delete the message for everyone. Admins can delete a message another member sent, for about two days after it was sent. It's replaced with "This message was deleted by admin [admin name]", so members see which admin did it, and it can't be recovered (WhatsApp Help Center).
  • Turn on Send for admin review. Click the group name, then Group permissions > Send for admin review > On. Everyone in the group gets a notice when you switch it on or off, and review requests expire after about two days (WhatsApp Help Center).
  • Let only admins post. Click the group name, then Group permissions, and turn off Send new messages. Only admins can then send messages in the group (WhatsApp Help Center). WhatsApp added this setting in 2018 for announcement groups such as schools and community centers (WhatsApp blog). It applies to every member, not one person.
  • Remove the member. WhatsApp's own advice for a disruptive member is that admins can remove them from the group (WhatsApp Help Center).

A short note to the person first often works better than any of these, and it keeps the group from seeing a public "deleted by admin" line under their name.

Keeping track of what matters in a muted group

Once a group is muted, the messages you do need get buried with the rest. Search the group for a word you expect, use the mentions filter, or see finding important messages in a WhatsApp group.
