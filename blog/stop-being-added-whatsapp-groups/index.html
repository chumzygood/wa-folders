How to stop people adding you to WhatsApp groups

Published November 19, 2026 · Em português

WhatsApp's default lets anyone who has your phone number add you to a group, including people who aren't in your contacts (WhatsApp Help Center). That is how the school group, the sales group and the group for a party you never agreed to go to all land in your chat list. One person on r/digitalminimalism put it this way: "I'm on too many groups, which I've attempted to deal with by archiving" (Reddit).

A setting called Groups, under Privacy, decides who can add you. You change it on your phone. WhatsApp says group privacy can't be changed on WhatsApp Web or the desktop app, but the setting you pick on the phone syncs to both (WhatsApp Help Center).

Change who can add you

  1. Open WhatsApp on your phone and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Privacy > Groups. On older versions of the app, Privacy sits under Account (WhatsApp blog).
  3. Pick My contacts or My contacts except.... If you pick the second one, search for or select the contacts you want to exclude.
  4. Tap Done if the app asks.

What each option does

  • Everyone. Anyone, including people outside your phone's address book, can add you without asking. This is the default.
  • My contacts. Only people saved in your address book can add you directly. Anyone else gets a pop-up saying they can't add you, and can send you a private group invite in a one-to-one chat instead. You have three days to accept it before it expires.
  • My contacts except... Same as My contacts, minus the people you exclude. An excluded person who tries to add you gets the same pop-up and can only send an invite.

All three are described on the same Help Center page. There is no Nobody option. WhatsApp offered one when the setting first rolled out in April 2019, then replaced it with My contacts except..., which lets you exclude specific contacts or "select all" (WhatsApp blog). If you exclude all your contacts, nobody can add you directly, and anyone who wants you in a group has to send an invite you can accept or ignore.

What the setting doesn't stop

  • Invite links. The setting only applies when someone adds you directly. People can still send you a group invite link, and you decide whether to join.
  • Groups you're already in. Changing the setting only affects new groups. You stay in every group you're in now until you exit each one.
  • Community announcements. If you're in a community, you're always added to its Announcements group, whatever your setting says.
  • Future adds after you leave. Exit group takes you out of that one group. It doesn't stop anyone adding you to the next one.

Each of these comes from WhatsApp's group privacy page. If you do decide to leave some of the groups you're in, leaving a group quietly covers what the admins and other members will see.

If one person keeps adding you

Choose My contacts except... and exclude that person. WhatsApp says this stops them adding you to groups from now on. Blocking them also works and goes further, because it stops all communication, group adds included. You can't block a group as such, but you can exit it and block the admin who added you (WhatsApp Help Center). Our post on blocking someone on WhatsApp Web has those steps.

If a number you don't know adds you

When someone outside your contacts adds you to a group, WhatsApp shows you more context about the group before you get into it. On WhatsApp Web and the desktop app you can click More or Group info to see details, Safety tools for tips, Ok to stay, or Exit group to leave (WhatsApp Help Center). When Meta introduced this screen in August 2025, it said you can leave without ever looking at the chat, and that the group's notifications stay silenced until you say you want to stay (Meta newsroom).

For the groups you keep

Some groups you can't leave, like the class group or the building group. To make them quieter, mute the chat for good or archive it (how archiving works on WhatsApp Web). To catch the one message that matters in a busy group, see finding important messages in a WhatsApp group.
