WhatsApp view-once text messages: how to send one, and whether WhatsApp Web can open it

Published November 12, 2026

WhatsApp has let you send photos, videos and voice messages as view once for a while. Now it is adding the same option for plain text. According to WABetaInfo, view-once text started reaching some Android beta testers on WhatsApp beta 2.26.45.6, and it will reach more accounts over the coming weeks. WhatsApp hasn't said when everyone gets it.

The short version: type your message, press and hold the send button, and pick View once. The other person can open it one time, can't screenshot or copy it, and then it's gone from both sides of the chat.

How to send a view-once text message on Android

If your account has the feature, WhatsApp shows a small tip at the bottom of the chat the first time, telling you to press and hold send. The steps, as reported by WABetaInfo and Digit:

  1. Open the chat and type your message as usual.
  2. Don't tap send. Press and hold the green send button instead.
  3. A small menu appears with Schedule message and View once.
  4. Tap View once. If an explanation sheet appears, tap OK. The message goes out right away.

You can't do both at once: a view-once text can't be scheduled.

If you hold the send button and only see the schedule option, or nothing at all, your account doesn't have it yet. Being on the beta doesn't guarantee it either, because WhatsApp turns it on for accounts in batches. If you want to try the beta anyway, here's how to join or rejoin the WhatsApp beta.

iPhone support is still in development. MacRumors reported in June that WhatsApp was building it for iOS too, for individual chats and groups but not channels.

What the other person sees

The message doesn't show up as normal text in the chat. Per WABetaInfo, the recipient has to tap it, and it opens on a separate screen. Once they close that screen, it can't be opened again. The chat then shows a marker that it was opened.

While it's open, WhatsApp blocks:

  • screenshots and screen recordings inside the app
  • copying the text
  • forwarding it to another chat
  • saving it

The tap-and-hold menu doesn't appear on it at all, so those options simply aren't there.

If the recipient has read receipts on, you can see when they opened it. After that, neither of you can read it again.

Is it actually private?

It's more private than a normal message, but don't treat it as a vault. WhatsApp's own help page on view once media says to only send view-once content to people you trust. It points out that someone can still take a photo of the screen with another phone before the message disappears. WABetaInfo makes the same point about text.

Two more details from that help page apply to view once media today. WhatsApp hasn't published separate rules for text yet, so assume they carry over until it does:

  • If someone reports a view-once message, a copy goes to WhatsApp with the report.
  • Unopened view-once media expires from the chat after 14 days.

Good uses: a Wi-Fi password, a door code, a one-off account number someone needs to type in once. Bad uses: anything you'd be in trouble over if it were photographed.

One more thing. If you just want a whole chat to clean itself up over time, that's a different feature. Disappearing messages delete everything in a chat after 24 hours, 7 days or 90 days, and the text stays readable until then.

Can you open a view-once text on WhatsApp Web?

Probably not, at least at first. WhatsApp hasn't said how view-once text will behave on WhatsApp Web or the desktop apps. What we do know is how it handles view-once photos, videos and voice messages there.

WhatsApp's help center says opening view once media isn't available on the web app. When one arrives, WhatsApp Web shows a note telling you to open it on your phone. TechCrunch reported in 2024 that view once was designed to work only in the Android and iOS apps, after a bug let people save view-once media through the web app. Linked phones hit the same wall: in an r/whatsapp thread, a user got the "open it on your phone" note on their second phone, and a reply explained that view once only opens on the main phone, not on linked devices.

The reason is the screenshot block. WhatsApp can stop screenshots inside its phone apps, but it can't stop the screenshot tool on your computer. So if view-once text follows the media rules, expect this:

  • You'll see a placeholder on WhatsApp Web, not the text.
  • You'll need to open it on your main phone.
  • Opening it there uses up the one view.

If you run most of your chats from a computer, keep that in mind before asking someone to send you a code this way. If you're the sender and the other person lives on WhatsApp Web, a normal message they can delete may work better for them.

Quick answers

Will it show in my backup? For view once media, WhatsApp says an unopened item can be restored from a backup, but an opened one can't. WhatsApp hasn't confirmed that text works the same way.

Can I send it in a group? MacRumors reported groups are planned. Check by holding the send button in a group chat once your account gets the feature.
