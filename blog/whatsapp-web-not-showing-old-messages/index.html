WhatsApp Web not showing older messages? Why days go missing

Published November 4, 2026

WhatsApp Web shows older messages only if your phone synced them to it, and WhatsApp says not every message and chat gets synced to a linked device, so your phone is the one place guaranteed to have the full history.

People run into this in two ways. A chat on WhatsApp Web stops scrolling back at some point, as if it began last month. Or the chat scrolls back fine, but a stretch of days is missing in the middle while the phone shows those messages.

How WhatsApp Web gets your history

WhatsApp Web is a linked device. On its page About message history on linked devices, checked on November 4, 2026, WhatsApp says:

  • Right after you link a device, your phone sends it an end-to-end encrypted copy of your most recent message history, and that copy is stored on the linked device.
  • The history can take a few minutes to appear on the linked device.
  • Not all messages and chats are synced to linked devices.
  • WhatsApp Desktop syncs more message history than WhatsApp Web.
  • To see or search your full message history, check your phone.

WhatsApp's About linked devices page also carried a notice on November 4, 2026: "There is a known issue for some linked devices not displaying up to one year of chat history." The same notice says WhatsApp is working on a fix and that you can still see your chat history on your primary device.

WhatsApp doesn't say how much history Web receives, or why one chat might lose a block of days while the days around it are there. So this post doesn't guess at a number.

What to try

  1. Wait a few minutes after linking. If you linked WhatsApp Web today, the copy from your phone may still be arriving. WhatsApp says this can take a few minutes.
  2. Update WhatsApp on your phone. WhatsApp's linked devices page says updates fix bugs and asks you to keep the app updated. With a known history issue open, an update is the cheapest thing to try first.
  3. Try WhatsApp Desktop. WhatsApp says the Desktop app syncs more message history than WhatsApp Web. If you need older messages on a computer, the Windows or Mac app is the official option that gets more of them.
  4. Use your phone for anything older. Search the chat on your phone. WhatsApp's own advice for the full history is to check your phone.

One more check if messages vanished instead of never showing up: WhatsApp says deleting a message for yourself or for everyone syncs across your devices that are online. A message deleted on your phone also leaves WhatsApp Web.

Keep a copy of a chat with the missing days

If you need those messages as a file, export from the phone. The Android steps on WhatsApp's export page are More options > More > Export chat, then Without media or Include media. WhatsApp notes the export is a text file, not a backup, so it can't be imported back.

WhatsApp Web has its own Export chat with All messages or Custom date range, saved as a ZIP without media. If the days you need are missing on Web, the phone export is the one to use. FAQ

Why does WhatsApp Web not show my old messages?

WhatsApp Web is a linked device and gets a copy of your recent history from your phone when you link it. WhatsApp says not all messages and chats are synced to linked devices, so older messages may never reach Web. Your phone keeps the full history.

Why are some days missing in a WhatsApp Web chat?

WhatsApp doesn't explain gaps of specific days. It says not all messages sync to linked devices, and its linked devices page notes a known issue where some linked devices don't show up to one year of chat history. Check the same chat on your phone, where WhatsApp says the history is still visible.

Will WhatsApp Desktop show more messages than WhatsApp Web?

WhatsApp says WhatsApp Desktop syncs more message history than WhatsApp Web. It doesn't say how much more, so it may still not show everything your phone has.

Does logging out and back in to WhatsApp Web bring old messages back?

WhatsApp doesn't document that as a fix. Linking again gives Web a new copy of your recent history from the phone, and WhatsApp still says not every message is synced, so don't count on it for older days.

How do I save the messages WhatsApp Web is missing?

Export the chat from your phone. On Android it's More options > More > Export chat, then choose with or without media. The file is a text export, not a backup, and can't be imported back into WhatsApp.
