How to schedule a message on WhatsApp

Published October 13, 2026 · Updated November 24, 2026

People have asked WhatsApp for a "send later" button for years. Telegram has had one since 2019. In October 2026, WhatsApp started rolling the feature out to some Android testers, then to some iPhone testers later that month. Meta has not announced it, and it may not appear on every account yet.

How to schedule a message

WABetaInfo documented the steps on the Android and iPhone apps:

  1. Open a chat and type your message, or choose one photo or video.
  2. Press and hold the send button.
  3. If the feature is on your account, a date and time picker appears. Choose when to send it and confirm.

WhatsApp sends the message at that time even if your phone is offline or the app is closed. The message stays end-to-end encrypted.

How far ahead, and what you can send

The earliest slot is 10 minutes from now. The latest is two weeks out. Those limits come from WABetaInfo's Android and iOS write-ups.

You can schedule text, links, photos and videos. Photos and videos go one at a time, so a second picture needs its own scheduled message. It works in one-to-one chats and in groups. In a group, only people who were already members when you scheduled the message will receive it. Someone who joins later will not.

After you schedule one, a scheduled messages section in the chat info screen (or the group info screen) lists it. You can view and delete it before it sends. The other person is not told that you scheduled it, and they are not told if you delete it.

Who has it

WABetaInfo first saw it in WhatsApp for Android 2.26.40.7 on October 12, 2026, then in WhatsApp for iPhone 26.42.10.19 on October 22. In both cases it was reaching some testers and rolling out to more accounts over the following weeks. WhatsApp has not said when everyone will get it. If you don't see the option after you press and hold send, the feature may not have reached your account yet.

You can receive a scheduled message even if your own app can't send one.

WhatsApp Web

WABetaInfo's articles cover the Android and iPhone apps. As of November 24, 2026, we could not find a WABetaInfo post or a WhatsApp Help Center page that documents scheduled messages on WhatsApp Web, so this page does not treat a Web how-to as confirmed. If you don't have the option yet, type the message and leave it in the box. WhatsApp Web keeps the draft in that chat.

Two Brazilian news sites did test it on the Web. On November 19, 2026, g1 and CNN Brasil reported a clock icon in the WhatsApp Web message box on some accounts in Brazil: type the message, click the clock, pick a day and time. Both could schedule text and images, but not voice notes or documents. Our Portuguese guide covers those tests.

What it doesn't do

WABetaInfo lists text, links, photos and videos. It does not mention voice notes, documents or recurring messages, so don't count on those.

WhatsApp Business has a separate Schedule control for marketing messages, described in the WhatsApp Help Center. That is a paid broadcast tool, and the phone has to be online at the send time. It is not the same as this chat feature.

If you wanted a reminder, not a scheduled send

Scheduling is for a message you have already written and want to go out later. A different case is a message you already sent, when you want to know if they never replied. Our guide to WhatsApp Web follow-up reminders covers that. WA Folders does not schedule or send messages.

FAQ

How do I schedule a message on WhatsApp?

On the Android and iPhone apps, type the message (or pick one photo or video), then press and hold the send button. If the feature is on your account, you can pick a date and time.

How far ahead can I schedule a WhatsApp message?

WABetaInfo says the earliest time is 10 minutes from now and the latest is two weeks ahead.

Can I schedule photos and videos?

Yes. WABetaInfo says you can schedule text, links, photos and videos, with one photo or video per scheduled message.

Does scheduled messaging work on WhatsApp Web?

As of November 24, 2026, we could not find that documented on WABetaInfo or in the WhatsApp Help Center. If you don't see an option, leave the text as a draft in the chat.

Why don't I see the option?

Meta has not announced the feature, and WhatsApp is rolling it out gradually. It may not appear on your account yet. That does not mean your app is broken.

Does WA Folders schedule or send messages?

No. It never types or sends a message. If you want to be reminded when someone hasn't replied, use a follow-up reminder and write the message yourself when it comes due.
