Can a team share one WhatsApp Business number?

Published November 22, 2026 · Em português

Yes. The WhatsApp Business app lets one phone and up to four linked devices use the same business account at the same time. WhatsApp says linking devices is there so more employees can answer customer messages (WhatsApp Help Center). A shop can keep the business phone at the counter, have WhatsApp Web open on two computers in the back, and put the app on a colleague's phone.

Every device is the same account. Everyone sees every chat, and anyone can reply as the business.

What can be linked

  • The primary phone. It holds the registration, and new devices are linked from it (WhatsApp Help Center).
  • Up to four linked devices. WhatsApp Web, WhatsApp Desktop, other phones and Android tablets can all be linked by scanning a QR code from the primary phone (WhatsApp Help Center). Since April 2023 an employee can answer customers from their own phone under the same WhatsApp Business account (WhatsApp blog).

Link a teammate's computer

With a QR code. On the computer, open web.whatsapp.com. On the business phone, open Linked devices (on iPhone: Settings > Linked devices > Link device; on Android: the menu > Linked devices > Link a device) and scan the code on the screen.

With the phone number, when the phone isn't next to the computer. This works on WhatsApp Web only (WhatsApp Help Center):

  1. On web.whatsapp.com, click Link with phone number. If the browser is signed in to a regular WhatsApp account, log out of it first.
  2. Choose the country, type the business number and click Next. An 8-character code appears.
  3. On the business phone, tap the Enter code notification, check the device name, tap Confirm and type the code.

Our post on using WhatsApp Web on two computers covers what each linked computer keeps for itself.

The rules that catch teams out

  • The business phone has to be used. Linked devices keep working while the phone is offline, but they log out if the phone goes unused for more than 14 days (WhatsApp Help Center). A phone left in a drawer over a holiday can sign out the whole team.
  • Idle devices drop off. WhatsApp disconnects a linked device after 30 days of inactivity (WhatsApp Help Center), so a computer used only in the busy season will need linking again.
  • Some features stay on the phone. Linked devices can't share or view live location, create or view broadcast lists, or add and delete status updates (WhatsApp Help Center).
  • Older history may be missing. WhatsApp lists a known issue where some linked devices don't show up to a year of chat history. The primary phone still has it (WhatsApp Help Center).
  • Someone leaves the team. On the business phone, open Linked devices, tap their device and choose Log out. WhatsApp also suggests checking the list regularly and removing any device you don't recognize (WhatsApp Help Center).

Stop two people answering the same customer

Without a paid plan, the app doesn't assign chats to people, so the team needs its own rule. Two that work on a small team:

  • A list per person or per stage. In the WhatsApp Business app, labels are now called lists. Tap Tools > Lists > New list, name it (a teammate's name, or "Waiting on payment"), then tap and hold a chat and choose Add to list. You can make up to 20 (WhatsApp Help Center).
  • Who covers when. Write down who answers in which hours, and agree that whoever replies first keeps the chat until it's closed.

More than four linked devices: multi-agent

WhatsApp sells a step up called multi-agent. With a Meta Verified or Meta One subscription, the business can link up to 10 devices (the exact number depends on the plan), name each device after the person using it, assign chats to agents and see which agent and device sent a message. WhatsApp notes that the premium version may not be available to you yet (WhatsApp Help Center).

  • Assign a chat. In the Chats tab, tap and hold a chat, pick any others, then choose Assign chat and select an agent. Each agent can filter the list to Assigned to you. Customers don't see which agent replied or who their chat is assigned to (WhatsApp Help Center).
  • The catches. With more than four linked devices, calls ring only on the primary phone. Tablets aren't supported. If the subscription ends or its benefits change, only the four most recently active devices stay linked (WhatsApp Help Center).

Past that, Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform offers paid messaging APIs with routing to live agents and CRM integrations, set up by your own developers or through a partner (WhatsApp Business).
