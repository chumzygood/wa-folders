WhatsApp account banned? How to request a review, and what happens next

Published November 24, 2026

If WhatsApp opens to "This phone number can't use WhatsApp" followed by your number, your account is banned. Tap Request review in the app, once, and wait. WhatsApp says most reviews finish within 24 hours and it sends you a notification with the decision. If there is no Request review option on that screen, WhatsApp says the ban is final and can't be appealed.

That is the whole official process. The rest of this post covers what each screen means, what to do while you wait, and the mistakes that make things worse. Everything here comes from WhatsApp's Help Center and the reports linked below, read on November 24, 2026.

Banned, temporarily banned or restricted?

WhatsApp uses three different messages, and they need different responses.

  • Banned. The full-screen "This phone number can't use WhatsApp" message. You can't use the account or reach your chats until the ban is lifted. WhatsApp's account bans page says it bans accounts it believes broke its Terms of Service, for example through spam, scams or putting other users' safety at risk.
  • Temporarily banned. WhatsApp's temporary ban page says this message usually means you are using an unofficial version of WhatsApp or scraping, meaning automatically collecting other users' numbers, photos or statuses. If you don't stop, the account can be banned permanently.
  • Restricted. "Your account is restricted right now" is a timed limit, not a ban. According to WhatsApp, it ends on its own, and in the meantime you can still message people you have chatted with before but can't start new chats, create groups or use linked devices such as WhatsApp Web. We wrote up what a restriction does to WhatsApp Web separately.

If your phone app works normally and only WhatsApp Web stopped, you are not banned. Check for a restriction first.

How to request a review

  1. Open WhatsApp on the phone that shows the ban message.
  2. Tap Request review.
  3. Gadgets 360 reports that the app then asks you to pick one of four review reasons. Choose the one that fits and tap Request review again.
  4. Leave it. The screen changes to show the account is under review, with the date you asked. WhatsApp notifies you when it has decided, and you can check the status at any time by opening the app.

WhatsApp is clear about what doesn't help. Contacting it outside the review, or through a friend's account, won't speed up the review or change the result. It reviews one phone number per appeal. And no outside service can ban or unban you: only WhatsApp can. Anyone selling an "unban" is selling nothing.

If you just got a new number

WhatsApp's ban page says that if your phone number is new to you, the previous owner may have broken the rules and got the number banned. Request a review anyway. It is the only route WhatsApp offers, and the review is about the number.

If there is no Request review button

WhatsApp says the ban is then final. The Gadgets 360 guide says the remaining option is a new number and a fresh account.

Some people in an October r/whatsapp thread with this exact message say they could register the same number again about two months later. WhatsApp's help pages don't say this anywhere, so treat it as people's experience, not a rule.

If Request review says "try again later"

People have been posting on X this week that the button fails or won't submit, like this reply to @WhatsApp. WhatsApp's help pages don't mention this error. A few low-risk things to try:

  • Update WhatsApp from the Play Store or App Store. WhatsApp's ban page says it doesn't support out-of-date versions.
  • Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data and tap the button again later in the day, not twenty times in a row.
  • Keep the app installed. Gadgets 360 warns that uninstalling can delete a backup stored on the phone.

Don't install the app on another phone to try from there. WhatsApp reviews one number per appeal, so a second phone gains you nothing.

What happens to your chats

WhatsApp's ban page says you can't open your chat history or backups while the account is banned. A backup tied to that number only becomes usable after the number is unbanned. This is the main reason not to delete the app or wipe the phone while you wait.

Gadgets 360 adds that people can't message you after a ban and that a banned user is removed from groups after about 30 days. Those details come from its reporting, not from WhatsApp's help pages.

If the review goes your way

WhatsApp's unbanned page says you'll see "This account can now use WhatsApp." Verify your phone number as you did when you first set up WhatsApp. Your backup becomes usable again once the number is unbanned, and you can link WhatsApp Web again after that.

How to avoid a second ban

From WhatsApp's responsible use guide and its unofficial apps page:

  • Use the official app. GB WhatsApp, FM WhatsApp, TeleMessage, TM WhatsApp and other modified versions break the Terms and can get an account temporarily or permanently banned, or blocked from linking devices. To switch, uninstall the unofficial app, download WhatsApp from whatsapp.com and register your number again. WhatsApp can't promise chats from the unofficial app will carry over.
  • No bulk or automatic messaging. WhatsApp says not to bulk message, auto-message or auto-dial, and that it uses machine learning and user reports to find and ban accounts that send automated messages. Its rule doesn't make an exception for browser tools that send from WhatsApp Web.
  • Message people who expect you. Don't use contact lists you didn't build, and ask before adding people to groups.
  • Go easy on broadcast lists. WhatsApp says heavy broadcast use leads people to report you, and it bans accounts reported many times.

We covered the WhatsApp Plus question in detail in the official subscription vs the APK.

WhatsApp Business accounts

The Business app ban page works the same way: tap Request review, and no button means the ban is final. A banned Business account also loses its Meta Verified subscription automatically, and if the ban was for breaking the Meta Verified or business messaging rules, WhatsApp says there is no refund.
