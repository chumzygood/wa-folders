What WA Folders Pro (boulify) covers

Their listing covers four tools: folders, notes, privacy blur and quick-reply templates. It describes color-coded folder tabs and drag-and-drop filing. Its free version blurs profile photos; its Pro plan blurs messages and whole conversations, with hover to reveal. The listing says no account is required and nothing is sent to external servers.

Where WA Folders does more

WA Folders can search chat names, your notes and messages it has seen, and export the open chat to HTML, CSV, Markdown or JSON. It has reply follow-ups, including a reminder that clears when the person answers, and it can turn voice notes into text on your computer. The free plan includes 10 folders, against 3 in their free version. Our Pro price is published: $4.99 once. Their listing does not state a Pro price.

Is WA Folders Pro the paid plan of WA Folders?
