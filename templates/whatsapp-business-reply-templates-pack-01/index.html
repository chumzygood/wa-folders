About this pack

WA Folders Template Pack 01 is a set of 44 ready-to-edit reply messages for small businesses that answer customers on WhatsApp. It comes as a Markdown guide and a CSV file. Each row has a section, a suggested shortcut, a title, a suggested Meta template category and the message text. You copy the messages into quick replies in the WhatsApp Business app yourself. The pack does not install anything and does not send messages for you.

What's inside

  • Greetings and away messages (closed now, weekend, holiday, busy day)
  • Order messages (received, confirm details, ready, shipped, delayed, out of stock, cancelled)
  • Booking messages (confirmed, reminder, time slots, reschedule, missed appointment)
  • Payment messages (payment details, received, due soon, overdue, deposit, refund)
  • Follow-ups (quote, delivery check-in, unfinished order, back in stock, closing the loop)
  • Review asks and complaint replies
  • FAQ answers (prices, delivery, location, returns, payment methods, catalog, opt-out)

Every message has a suggested shortcut and fill-in blanks like {{name}} and {{order_id}}. If you use the WhatsApp Business Platform (API), each message is also tagged with a suggested Meta template category (Utility, Marketing, or Service when no template is needed), with a short guide to the rules.

These are one-to-one replies, not broadcast or bulk messages.

You get

  • A guide with setup steps for quick replies in the WhatsApp Business app
  • A CSV file with all 44 messages to edit in a spreadsheet before you paste them in
