What you can check for yourself

  • The Chrome Web Store listing shows the current version (1.3.8, updated October 22, 2026) and the permissions it asks for.
  • The only site it asks to run on is web.whatsapp.com. AI and license sites are optional permissions you approve only if you use those features.
  • The 33-second demo is a recording of the real extension, using sample chats with made-up names.
  • There is no WA Folders account to create, and no WA Folders server that holds your chats.

What makes it different

WhatsApp's own Lists filter chats on your phone and on the web. WA Folders adds things Lists don't: reply tracking, private notes, search across notes, and export to four formats, all on WhatsApp Web in Chrome. See the side-by-side comparison.

Questions

Is WA Folders free?

Yes. The free plan has no time limit: 5 folders, 20 search results per search, 3 exports a day, 5 voice-note transcriptions and 20 translations a day. Pro removes those limits for a one-time $4.99.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. You pay $4.99 once through Polar and Pro stays unlocked.

Does it work on the WhatsApp phone app?

No. WA Folders runs only on WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com) in Chrome on a computer. Folders and notes don't appear on your phone.

Where is my data stored?

In chrome.storage.local in your browser. There is no WA Folders server or account. Data leaves the browser only for features you turn on, like Chrome Sync (Pro, off by default) or an AI provider key you add yourself.

Is WA Folders made by WhatsApp?

No. It is an independent Chrome extension, not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
