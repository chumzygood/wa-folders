Older messages and Load whole chat

Leave a chat open and WA Folders quietly adds its older messages to search. Pro can load the whole chat at once.

10-second silent demo with captions (also as a caption track, in English and Portuguese). Recorded on WA Folders 2.4.1 with made-up chats.

Why it helps

Search only finds what has been loaded. This fills in older history without you scrolling up by hand.

How to use it

  1. Open a chat and leave it open. WA Folders loads older pages after about 45 idle seconds.
  2. Check the Older messages line under Search to see its status.
  3. Pro: press Load whole chat in This chat to load the full history for search and export.

Free or Pro

Automatic older-message search: Free and Pro. Load whole chat: Pro. WhatsApp may not return every old message.

WA Folders works on WhatsApp Web in Chrome, Edge and Firefox on a computer. Your folders, notes and search index stay in this browser by default, and WA Folders does not press Send for you.
