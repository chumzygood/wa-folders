Where Cooby does more

Cooby's listing is built around three jobs WA Folders does not do: syncing WhatsApp to a CRM, a team inbox, and sending to many chats at once. It can show a HubSpot contact card inside WhatsApp, log messages to HubSpot, broadcast, and start a chat with a number that is not in your contacts. Paid team plans on cooby.co/pricing add a shared inbox, multiple WhatsApp numbers, and message syncing (6,000 synced messages per user per month on Starter, billed at $15.99 per user per month when paid yearly). The listing also includes live chat sharing and Google Calendar events.

Where WA Folders does more

WA Folders has no account and keeps folders, notes and the search index in Chrome on your computer. It can search message text it has already seen, and export a chat to HTML, CSV, Markdown or JSON. It can turn voice notes into text on your computer. It never sends a message for you. Pro is $4.99 once, with no subscription.

How to pick

  • Your team uses HubSpot or Salesforce, or you need broadcasts: Cooby.
  • You are one person who wants folders, search and export in the browser: WA Folders.
  • You do not want a subscription: the WA Folders paid plan is $4.99 once. Cooby's team plans are billed monthly or yearly.
  • You do not want any tool to send messages for you: WA Folders.

FAQ

Is WA Folders a Cooby alternative?

Only for tabs, notes and reminders. Cooby is a team CRM and messaging tool. WA Folders does not sync to a CRM and never sends messages.

Does Cooby keep chats on my computer?

Its Chrome Web Store listing says chat data never leaves your computer. Its pricing page lists message syncing to HubSpot or Salesforce on paid team plans, including 6,000 synced messages per user per month on Starter.

How much is Cooby?

The free extension plan includes 3 custom tabs, 3 templates and 5 scheduled events a month. Team plans start at $15.99 per user per month when billed yearly, which the pricing page shows as $191.88 a year.

Does WA Folders work for a team?

There is no team inbox, no shared account and no CRM sync. Each person would use their own Chrome profile. Folders do not appear on the phone.

Can I use both?

Both add tabs above the WhatsApp Web chat list, so they may clash. We have not tested them together.
