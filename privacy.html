WA Folders privacy policy

Effective: October 14, 2026 · Applies to: the WA Folders Chrome extension (version 1.2.9 and later) and the website wafolders.com.

The short version

  • WA Folders has no account and no server. Your folders, notes, reminders, search index and settings are stored in your Chrome profile on your computer.
  • We don't collect, sell or share your chats. The extension has no analytics, ads or trackers and sends nothing to us. The website wafolders.com uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits; it sets no cookies and keeps no personal data.
  • Data leaves your browser only for a feature you turn on, and only to the service that feature needs: Polar (license check), Chrome Sync (Pro sync, off by default), an AI or speech provider whose key you added, or Hugging Face (one-time speech model download).
  • WA Folders never sends WhatsApp messages for you.

What the extension reads

WA Folders runs only on https://web.whatsapp.com. To show folders, markers, search and exports, it reads what WhatsApp Web shows on your screen: chat names, chat ids, message text and times in chats you open, and whether a message is unread or from you. It does not read your phone, your contacts list outside WhatsApp Web, or other websites.

The website (wafolders.com)

wafolders.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. The website (not the extension) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It sets no cookies and keeps no personal data. The site runs no ad scripts. The WA Folders extension itself still sends nothing to us. Like any web host, GitHub (and the domain's DNS provider) may log technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site; see GitHub's privacy statement. Links to the Chrome Web Store and Polar carry campaign tags (utm_…) that those services may record.

Chrome Web Store and Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs and from WhatsApp Web pages will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. WA Folders uses data only to provide the features you see. It does not sell data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or let humans read it. Data is transferred only as described above, to provide a feature you chose, or where the law requires.

The Chrome Web Store itself (Google) handles installs, reviews and the store's own install statistics under Google's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

  • Local data stays until you delete it. Delete a note, reminder or folder in the panel, or remove everything by uninstalling WA Folders (Chrome deletes its local storage). Export a backup first in Settings → Backup & restore if you want a copy.
  • Synced data: turn Sync off in WA Folders settings, or clear it from Chrome Sync in your Google account.
  • Polar keeps purchase and license records as a payment processor and under tax law. To ask about them, contact us or Polar.
  • We hold no copy of your chats, so there is nothing on our side to delete. For other questions about your data, email us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because WA Folders stores your data on your own device, you can do this directly. For purchase records held by Polar, email us and we'll help.

Children

WA Folders is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we don't knowingly collect their data. WhatsApp itself sets a minimum age to use its service.

Security

Data stays in Chrome's extension storage. Password-protected exports use AES-GCM-256 with a PBKDF2-SHA-256 key (600,000 iterations); the PIN lock stores only a salted PBKDF2 hash.

AI agent access (version 1.3.1, off by default)

WA Folders can let an AI agent on your own computer (for example Claude Desktop, Cursor, Codex or another app that speaks the Model Context Protocol, "MCP") use folders you choose. It works only after you do all three: install the separate WA Folders agent bridge on your computer, turn on Settings > AI agent access (Chrome then asks for its "communicate with cooperating native applications" permission), and tick the folders the agent may use.

How it connects

The extension talks to the bridge through Chrome native messaging. The bridge is a small program on your computer that your AI app starts; the two connect over a local pipe on your machine only. The bridge makes no internet connections, and there is no WA Folders server in between.

What the agent can read

Only the folders you ticked. From those: folder names, chat names, your private notes, reminder times, and short snippets (the last few lines, up to 300 characters each) of messages WA Folders has already indexed on your computer. Each allowed folder can also be offered to the agent as a short summary ("resource", capped at about 4,000 tokens) so the agent has it as context. Chats in folders you did not tick, and chats in no folder, are never shared.

What the agent can do

Read-only by default. If you also turn on the separate write switch, the agent can add a chat to an allowed folder and set a WA Folders reminder. It can never send, read aloud, delete or open WhatsApp messages, and never presses Send.

Optional "AI summaries" toggle

Off by default. If you turn on the optional "AI summaries" toggle (Settings > AI agent access > Let the agent send chat summaries to my AI provider), then when the agent asks for a chat summary, that chat's name, your note on it and its last 8 messages are sent from your browser to the AI provider whose API key you entered in Private AI, and nowhere else. WA Folders does not receive or keep a copy, and your provider may charge your key for each summary. With the toggle off, no summary request is sent.

Retention, consent and control

  • WA Folders keeps nothing new: the bridge stores no data, and the agent settings stay on this computer (never synced or put in backups).
  • What you share with an agent is then handled by that agent's provider under its own privacy policy and retention rules.
  • Revoke anytime: Settings > Revoke all agent access turns it off, clears the folder list and removes Chrome's native-messaging permission. To remove the bridge, run the uninstall line its installer prints.
