Folders, search and follow-ups for WhatsApp Web

WA Folders is a Chrome extension for web.whatsapp.com. It adds folder tabs above your chat list, search across chats and notes, chat export, voice notes to text, message translation, and one line each morning that shows who you owe a reply and who owes you one. Everything is kept in your browser. There is no WA Folders account or server.

Add to Chrome, free Free vs Pro

Works on WhatsApp Web in Chrome on a computer. It does not work in the WhatsApp phone app. Current version: 1.2.6.

What it adds to WhatsApp Web

The WA Folders panel sits in the conversation area, so it doesn't cover your chat list. On a narrow window it folds into a tab.

Voice notes

Voice notes to text

Click Transcribe under a voice note to read it as text. By default the speech model runs on your computer and the audio stays there. The first time, WA Folders shows the download size and asks before it fetches the model once from Hugging Face. You can pick Accurate (about 252 MB) or Light (about 80 MB, better for slower computers or metered connections).

You can also use your own OpenAI or Google Gemini key; then the audio of the note you click goes from your browser to that provider. Transcripts stay on this device and show up in search and exports. Free: 5 transcriptions a day.

Translate

Translate messages: Portuguese, English, Spanish

Click Translate next to a message, or under a voice-note transcript, and the translation appears under it with the language it came from. If that language is wrong, click change.

Translate draft turns what you typed into Portuguese, English or Spanish in the message box, and Undo puts your original back. WA Folders never sends it; you press Enter yourself. Translation runs on your computer with Chrome's translator. Free: 20 translations a day.

Private AI

Catch me up, draft a reply

One click summarizes a long chat and lists what's still waiting on you. Another drafts a reply you can edit. It goes into the message box, and you press Enter yourself. Drafts follow the chat's language.

By default it runs on your own computer with Chrome's built-in AI (Chrome 138 or newer on a supported computer), so the chat never leaves it. Optional: use your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or local (Ollama) key. Then the messages of the chat you ask about go straight from your browser to that provider, never to us. Claude Opus 5.5 with your own Anthropic key is coming in 1.2.7.

Folders

Folder tabs above your chat list

Make folders like Family, Work or Clients, with your own colours and emoji. They show up as tabs above the chat list, with unread, reply, question and due markers. Click a chat in a folder and it opens.

On first use, WA Folders can suggest folders from the chat names on screen. Nothing is filed until you press Add these. The free plan includes 5 folders.

Today line

Waiting on them, and Questions for me

One line at the top of the panel shows what's due, who is waiting for your reply, who asked you a question, and who you're still waiting on.

Click it for two lists. Waiting on them shows one-to-one chats where you spoke last and heard nothing back. Questions for me shows chats whose last message asks you something.

Search

Search chats, notes and messages

Search chat names, including chats you haven't opened yet, plus your notes and the messages WA Folders has seen while you had chats open. Filter by folder, notes or links, and save a search.

The free plan shows 5 results per search. With Pro, press Load whole chat and search covers that chat's entire history.

Export

Export a chat to HTML, CSV, Markdown or JSON

Export the open chat in one click. CSV opens cleanly in Excel, including on Windows. Photos, files and voice messages are labelled, for example [photo] and [voice message].

Free: 3 exports a day of the messages loaded on screen. Pro exports the whole chat from the start, a whole folder in one file, or a password-protected file that opens in any browser with the password.

Notes and reminders

Private notes and follow-ups that clear themselves

Write a note on any chat, like "owes me $40". It is saved only on your computer.

Sent something and need an answer? Choose "Remind me if they don't reply in 1 day." If they answer, the reminder disappears. If they don't, the chat comes back under Due with a badge on the toolbar icon and an optional desktop notification. The free plan allows 2 reminders at a time.

Privacy blur

Blur your chats, or hide WhatsApp Web at once

Blur hides names, photos and messages. Hover over one to peek. Turn it on with the eye button in the panel or press Alt+Shift+U. You can change the key at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Alt+Shift+H hides WhatsApp Web at once. Pro adds a PIN lock screen for when you step away.

Add to Chrome, then upgrade

Install WA Folders, then press Upgrade in any limit message or in the toolbar popup. After checkout, paste your license key (it starts with WAF-) in the popup.

A few technical limits apply on every plan: Load whole chat stops at 20,000 messages, one export of the open chat keeps the newest 5,000 loaded messages, a whole-folder export opens up to 100 chats in one run, and voice notes longer than 15 minutes are not transcribed.

Privacy

Your folders, notes, reminders and search index are stored in chrome.storage.local in your Chrome profile on this computer.

  • No account, analytics, ads or trackers, and no WA Folders server.
  • It never sends messages for you. No bulk sending, broadcasts, auto-replies or contact scraping.
  • It does not use the WhatsApp Business API. It runs only on web.whatsapp.com.
  • AI, voice-note transcription and translation run on your computer by default. If you add your own API key, only the messages you ask about, the voice note you click Transcribe on, or the text you click Translate on when Chrome can't translate it go to the provider you chose, and only when you click.
  • Translations are shown on screen and never saved, searched or exported. Transcripts stay on this device.
  • If you buy Pro, the extension checks your license key. No chat content goes with it.
  • Sync (Pro, off by default) copies folders, quick replies and rules through Chrome Sync. Messages and notes are never synced.

Questions

How do I make folders in WhatsApp Web?

Install the WA Folders extension from the Chrome Web Store and open web.whatsapp.com. In the WA Folders panel, type a folder name and press Add. Then open a chat and press Add chat on that folder. Each folder appears as a tab above your chat list. The free plan includes 5 folders.

Can I search WhatsApp Web messages?

Yes. WA Folders searches chat names (including chats you haven't opened yet), your private notes, and messages in chats you have opened while it was running. You can filter by folder, notes or links. The free plan shows 5 results per search. Pro shows all results, and after Load whole chat it searches that chat's full history.

How do I find chats where I'm waiting on a reply?

Open the WA Folders panel on WhatsApp Web and click "waiting" in the Today line at the top. The Waiting on them list shows one-to-one chats where you sent the last message and got no reply. Next to it, Questions for me lists chats whose last message asks you something.

Can I export a WhatsApp Web chat?

Yes. Open the chat and press HTML, CSV, Markdown or JSON in the WA Folders panel. The file holds the messages loaded on screen, and photos, files and voice messages show as labels such as [photo] and [voice message]. The free plan allows 3 exports a day. Pro has no daily cap and can export the whole chat from the start, a whole folder in one file, or a password-protected file.

Can WA Folders transcribe WhatsApp Web voice notes?

Yes. Click Transcribe under a voice note to read it as text. By default the speech model runs on your computer and the audio stays there. The first time, WA Folders shows the download size and asks before it fetches the model once: Accurate (about 252 MB) or Light (about 80 MB). You can also use your own OpenAI or Google Gemini key. Transcripts stay on this device and show up in search and exports. The free plan includes 5 transcriptions a day.

Can it translate WhatsApp Web messages?

Yes, between Portuguese, English and Spanish. Click Translate next to a message, or under a voice-note transcript, and the translation appears under it. Translate draft turns what you typed into Portuguese, English or Spanish in the message box, and Undo puts your original back. It runs on your computer with Chrome's translator. WA Folders never sends it; you press Enter yourself. The free plan includes 20 translations a day.

Is it free?

Yes. The free plan has no time limit and includes 5 folders, 5 search results and 3 exports a day, plus 5 voice-note transcriptions and 20 translations a day and small allowances for reminders, quick replies, rules, AI answers and the deal board. Pro is unlimited and costs $9.99 a month or $99 for lifetime. You upgrade from inside the extension.

Does it work on the phone app?

No. WA Folders is a Chrome extension that runs only on WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com) in Chrome on a computer. Your folders and notes do not appear in the WhatsApp phone app.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser, in chrome.storage.local on your computer. WA Folders has no server and no account. AI, voice-note transcription and translation run on your computer by default. Data leaves the browser only for features you turn on: Chrome Sync if you enable sync (Pro), the provider you chose if you add your own API key (only what you click on), and the license check when you enter a Pro key (no chat content).

Does it send messages for me?

No. WA Folders never presses Send. Quick replies and AI drafts go into the message box and you press Enter yourself. It does no bulk sending or auto-replies, and it does not use the WhatsApp Business API.

Is WA Folders made by WhatsApp?

No. WA Folders is an independent extension. It is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
